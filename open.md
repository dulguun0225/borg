# Open

## Is supplying the fleet's credentials a duty?

Every agent stands on a provider account somebody owns, and [_The fleet_](how-humans-do-it/10-fleet.md) settles what the factory does when one is spent or withdrawn: it holds, and waits for a human to attach another. Which human has no duty number. The twelve are what a human does with the work, and standing the factory up sits outside them — [_The reconciler_](how-humans-do-it/08-operations.md#the-reconciler) leans on that precedent by name when it says installing one is done once and asks nothing after.

A credential does not hold still that way. A personal account expires, is revoked, and runs out, so what is being supplied is maintained rather than installed, and the factory stops until it is. Calling it a thirteenth duty costs what adding to that list always costs: every bare number in the tree is a duty number, and the list is cited from every section. Leaving it outside costs a standing obligation nobody holds — the People declaration records who lent a credential and enforces nothing, which is right for routing and thin for a factory that stalls without one.

What turns on it is also what happens when the row goes unanswered. A [_page_](how-humans-do-it/08-operations.md#pages) has no duty of its own and reaches whoever holds the duty the row belongs to, widening to every holder and then to the owner. A row belonging to no duty has nowhere to widen to, so an empty fleet entry waits on whoever notices it. That is tolerable while an owner is watching the factory they just stood up, and it is the failure mode of exactly the unproven early setup the personal account exists to serve.
