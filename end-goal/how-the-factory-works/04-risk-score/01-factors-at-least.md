# Factors, at least

Four groups of factor, named so a human can argue with them, and the set is open.

- **The change** — size, how much of the system it can affect, churn in the item's [area](../02-intent-into-items/03-decomposition/02-what-an-item-names.md), reversibility. Test coverage is deliberately not among them: it would be computed from the [encodings](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/05-implementation/README.md) the scored agent authored in the same [stage](../01-one-pipeline.md) as the code, so the scored author would set the factor. Assertions piled on accessors beside an untested payment path read as high coverage, so it would lower the number exactly when it was gamed. The [analysis window](../08-operations/02-the-analysis-window.md) would then close `passed`, the one exit taken on evidence, and the manoeuvre would narrow the prior of the author that performed it; or the window would close `timed out` on a quiet service, where nothing catches it and nothing learns from it either. It returns if something ever derives it independently of the author, the same condition [_Implementation_](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/05-implementation/README.md) states for making the encoding itself mechanical. Leaving it out costs this: the change group reads a tested change and an untested one alike; what carries that information instead is [the run](../05-environments/04-what-the-candidate-environment-decides/README.md) — a [criterion](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/02-spec/02-in-force-and-withdrawal.md) in force with no encoding naming it is already a rejection at [_Implementation_](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/05-implementation/README.md).

  [Mutation of the build](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/05-implementation/03-what-the-encoding-rests-on.md), which tests whether the encodings notice a seeded defect, is that derivation: it is a reading on the build rather than a factor of this group, decided at [_Merge to master_](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/07-merge-to-master.md) on the terms [_Implementation_](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/05-implementation/README.md) states.

- **The author** — the **per-author prior**, one per human and per AI model version, computed from that author's own work with the changes a human [undid after they shipped](../../what-humans-do.md) (10) and the [rollbacks](../06-releases/06-rollback.md) marked. A [revert's deploy](../08-operations/03-overlapping-windows.md) delivers several releases under one window, and that window's close moves no prior in either direction: one comparison over several changes is a label the evidence does not support, toward fault and toward trust alike. Only the release the [search](../08-operations/03-overlapping-windows.md) names takes a failed batch's outcome, and where no search runs or it names nobody, the batch teaches nothing — the reading an absent input already gets. A deploy has no author: the [deployer](../08-operations/01-the-health-monitor.md), the component that performs every deploy, is not one, so a deploy accrues no [attempt](../03-gates/05-the-attempt-limit.md) and moves no prior, and the outcome that does move one, the [window](../08-operations/02-the-analysis-window.md) closing on the release, is read against the artifact's author and never against what deployed it. A prior read as a list of only the bad outcomes could never narrow toward trust: what narrows it is the work nobody undid, so every outcome on that author's [artifact](../01-one-pipeline.md) moves it.

  Which [window](../08-operations/02-the-analysis-window.md) exits are outcomes is stated exit by exit and never as _closing without failing_, because three of the four close without failing and only one of them concludes anything. `passed` narrows the prior and `failed` widens it, each being a comparison that ruled something out. `timed out` and `skipped` move it in neither direction: a comparison the cap closed unresolved, and a release a rollback undid before anything measured it, each reports no outcome and so teaches the prior nothing. The [held-out sample](02-how-it-learns.md) gets the same silence in [_How it learns_](02-how-it-learns.md), where an exit reporting no outcome reports none there either. It also subsumes the window whose comparison received no volume at all — an absent input under [_The analysis window_](../08-operations/02-the-analysis-window.md)'s own reading, and now one case of a wider rule rather than the only exception to a looser one. Two outcomes are excluded for reasons of their own: a rollback a human [marked as not caused by the release](../08-operations/03-overlapping-windows.md), evidence about something other than the author; and [a queue rejection laid to something that moved under the candidate](../05-environments/03-the-merge-queue.md), a dependency's release or the [design-system record](../02-intent-into-items/01-intake/01-constraints-and-the-design-system.md) between the two runs, evidence about what moved and not the author.

  How wide the prior is, how far it sits from the trust an author's evidence could justify, follows the same distinction: it may not narrow past the width its own count of `passed` and `failed` closes supports. So a service too quiet to close a window on evidence keeps the prior it was installed with however many releases it has shipped, and windows that ruled nothing out buy no trust however many of them elapse. That width and that count are written on the vector beside the factor, so an owner reading a low number can see whether it was earned or waited out. What the two rules cost is the direction they leave: on a quiet service an author the factory has every reason to trust goes on being read as unfamiliar, and the human stays at the gate — weak protection reported as weak. That is what _The analysis window_ already accepts for a service whose traffic rules nothing out, and the trade the [held-out sample](02-how-it-learns.md) also takes.

  It is kept per model version and not per family, which is how a new version accumulates a prior of its own; the cost: moving a [fleet entry](../10-fleet/README.md) to a newer version starts its evidence over, and keeping the old name for a new version reads the old version's outcomes as the new one's. The [effort](../10-fleet/01-what-an-agent-runs-on.md) an entry runs at and the [role prompt](../10-fleet/03-what-an-agent-is-told/README.md) an agent works from change how one model behaves under one name too, and both are deliberately left out of the key — [_Paid for is not authored by_](../10-fleet/04-paid-for-is-not-authored-by.md) refuses the split and states what refusing it costs. Both are recorded on the vector a gate firing writes instead, which is where a fact a human argues with belongs. It starts wide for an author the factory has not seen and narrows with evidence, or starts at the prior the product shipped for that model version where it shipped one. [_A model under a name_](../10-fleet/02-a-model-under-a-name.md) sets that out, with what a name pinned at the provider is worth and with the one instrument the factory holds on a version that is not an outcome. A version the instrument finds departed from its own earlier results takes [the treatment a drifted factor takes](02-how-it-learns.md) — the prior resolved rather than weighed, a human deciding whatever the formula returns, because a prior earned before the departure is evidence about a different author.

  The restriction [_The analysis window_](../08-operations/02-the-analysis-window.md) keeps, that only a rollback or a human's undo traceable to the [health monitor](../08-operations/01-the-health-monitor.md) counts, is that window's parameters' rule and not this one's. The window learns from what the [health monitor](../08-operations/01-the-health-monitor.md) could have seen; the prior learns from every outcome on that author's work, a change undone for being the wrong feature included, because building the wrong thing well says something about the author and nothing about the health monitor.
- **Exposure** — what the change reaches that the service did not reach before, read from the same diff: an outbound call added, a credential named or read, an authorization check removed or weakened, and a dependency change. A dependency change is the [build](../05-environments/01-records-and-one-long-lived-branch.md)'s resolved set diffed against the set of the service's [current release](../06-releases/05-the-deploy-record/README.md)'s build, each package added or moved named with its version and its declared licence. An unpinned range that resolves differently with nothing in the manifest changed is read as the change it is. Three things separate it from the change group. It only ever raises the number: a diff adding none of that reads as nothing, and nothing in this group lowers a score, its absence not being evidence of safety. It does not learn from outcomes: the signals [_How it learns_](02-how-it-learns.md) reads are misbehaviour signals, and a change that posts credentials outward moves neither error rate nor latency, so a [window](../08-operations/02-the-analysis-window.md) closing `passed` on it says nothing about it. A release whose [security predicates](../05-environments/04-what-the-candidate-environment-decides/README.md) rejected nothing closes that same window the same way, an outcome that teaches this factor nothing either: the class those predicates do not cover stays exactly as blind. Its weight is published and calibrated like the rest, and the only outcome that ever speaks to it is a later [advisory](../02-intent-into-items/01-intake/03-advisories.md), report, or undo, arriving on the prior and not on this factor. And it reads the diff and never the intent: an injected change and a mistaken one add the same call, which is what makes it the one factor a stranger's sentence in a [report](../02-intent-into-items/01-intake/02-reports.md) cannot talk its way past.

  Its evidence is a list on the vector (each call, each credential name, each check, each package with its version and licence, and the file and line of each), so what the human at [_Implementation_](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/05-implementation/README.md) argues with is that list beside the diff [Work](../11-screens/01-work-ops-factory-people.md) renders. The list is not the diff, and where the two disagree the human decides over the code, the disagreement being what the list's derivation missed. It is derived by the factory from the diff and the build record, independent of the author, which is the condition test coverage failed above.

  What extracts a call or a credential read is per toolchain, as [_What a consumer declares_](../07-contracts/06-what-a-consumer-declares.md)'s derivation is, so where no extractor runs for a toolchain the factor is unavailable and resolves the way an unavailable factor does below (a human at the gate whatever the formula returns, the vector naming why), and never as nothing. What it costs: an extractor per toolchain to ship and keep, a human at Implementation on every change on a toolchain without one, and a residue the list states rather than closes — what an extractor misses is decided by nobody, and a change whose harm nothing observed narrows its author's prior like any other window that closed on evidence.
- **Context** — what this change touches in this customer's business, and which sibling services consume what it publishes. The same diff is a different risk in a payments path than on a marketing page. What says which of the two an item is in is the [hazard severity](../02-intent-into-items/03-decomposition/03-hazard-severity.md) of its area, this group's one declared input and the only term in the vector an owner writes rather than the factory derives. An `irreversible` value is resolved rather than weighed: at [_Implementation_](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/05-implementation/README.md) the decision is a human's whatever the formula returns, one the [held-out sample](02-how-it-learns.md) may not select past. That gate and not the three above it, because a human at all four would stop the factory authoring in such an area at all, and Implementation is where the diff and the exposure list are already in front of one. Not [_Deploy to production_](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/08-deploy-to-production.md) either: a human at every release of a service is what a [safeguard](../09-gate-policy/02-one-shape-across-all-of-them.md) already buys an owner who wants one. The [intent](../02-intent-into-items/01-intake/README.md)'s source is here too, and it is the second of the two values in this group resolved rather than weighed. An intent whose evidence carries text the factory did not author, that no gate has admitted, resolves the source value toward the [Spec](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/02-spec/README.md) gate: a human at the gate whatever the formula returns, one the [held-out sample](02-how-it-learns.md) may not select past, resolution being what it already never overrides. An intent grouped from [reports](../02-intent-into-items/01-intake/02-reports.md) is the existing case: that channel is the one way in the factory cannot authenticate, which [_Reports_](../02-intent-into-items/01-intake/02-reports.md) states and owns. Withdrawing a criterion whose [provenance](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/02-spec/01-the-record.md) is human-confirmed or constraint-derived is a resolved value at Spec, and so is a screen revision that removes a forbidden transition a human-confirmed machine declared, which [_In force, and withdrawal_](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/02-spec/02-in-force-and-withdrawal.md) and [_The screen state machine_](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/02-spec/04-the-screen-state-machine.md) state.

  The change group's reversibility factor takes that same resolution where the diff destroys stored data: resolved rather than valued, a human deciding at Implementation with the diff in front of them. Exposure above [the bound](../09-gate-policy/01-what-is-in-it.md) gate policy sets takes it too, the [held-out sample](02-how-it-learns.md) barred from selecting past it. What it costs: a fourth named resolution to publish and calibrate around, and a human at Implementation on every item whose diff destroys data or whose exposure crosses the bound.

The exposure factor reads a credential name added or removed in the service's configuration file the way it reads one added or removed in code, since a change to that file is a diff like any other.

A version of what an agent is told is scored on a set of its own, because the change group is computed from a diff and a [role prompt](../10-fleet/03-what-an-agent-is-told/README.md) has no code to have sized and no area to have churned. Three factors replace that group at [_A role prompt or a skill_](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/09-a-role-prompt-or-a-skill.md) — the share of the factory working from this version; **departure**, how far it differs from the version in force; and reversibility, which is high for every version, [withdrawal](../10-fleet/03-what-an-agent-is-told/README.md) being a second record and nothing having been deployed. The author and context groups are unchanged, the author being the agent that wrote the version. That the set is different here rather than partly unavailable is the point: a factor the score cannot compute puts a human at the gate, and treating area churn as missing rather than as inapplicable would put one at every version forever — a decision nobody made, arriving as a side effect. One version has no author at all — the [shipped entry](../10-fleet/03-what-an-agent-is-told/README.md) an upgrade's first start enters, install's own firing no gate — and that absence is resolved rather than classed: a human at the gate whatever the formula returns, decided here so that a narrow shipped skill cannot auto-pass at an upgrade as a side effect. What it costs: a second factor set to publish, calibrate, and argue with, on a row that fires far less often than the eight of the [default path](../03-gates/03-actions-at-each-gate.md).

Likelihood and impact stay separate until the last step. They answer different questions and call for different responses: likely-wrong but cheap to undo should ship and let rollback handle it; unlikely but catastrophic should be gated regardless. This is also why one score decides three things about a change: the gate reads mostly likelihood against impact, the [rollout strategy](../03-gates/02-the-rollout-strategy.md) reads mostly impact against reversibility and how fast a problem would appear, and the boundary of the [_analysis window_](../08-operations/02-the-analysis-window.md) reads mostly impact. Reading different halves is what keeps them from weakening together: a change misjudged on likelihood, which is what a per-author prior and an unfamiliar area affect, loses its gate and keeps its watch.

Cost is not a factor and not a strategy input. A cost term would have the score trade a gate or a [control](../08-operations/01-the-health-monitor.md) for money, which [_How it learns_](02-how-it-learns.md) already refuses for the threshold: loosening it on cost alone is a judgment about what the factory is for, and the design hands that to the owner (8). The strategy is the same case: what a control costs is the owner's to weigh, and the levers are theirs — the [window limit](../08-operations/03-overlapping-windows.md), the [fraction of its instances a release keeps](../08-operations/03-overlapping-windows.md), and a [safeguard](../09-gate-policy/02-one-shape-across-all-of-them.md) on the strategy. Model spend has a lever of its own in the [spend ceiling](../10-fleet/08-a-spend-ceiling.md) an owner authors on a credential, which reaches nothing a control costs. The second reason is that the score learns from outcomes its own decisions selected, and cost is not one of them: what a change spent says nothing about whether the gate that let it through was right. Where the owner has authored no rate, the price per unit recorded on the [_People_](../11-screens/01-work-ops-factory-people.md) declaration, there is a further reason: spend is recorded in the provider's units, which differ per model, so a factor over a fleet of two models would weigh units that do not add. What it costs: a large low-risk service pays a full control for every release the score gives one, and only a value an owner authored reduces it.

The defaults the score supplies where an owner authored nothing are not a fourth factor. [_Gate policy_](../09-gate-policy/02-one-shape-across-all-of-them.md)'s eleven parameters are each a field of the record its scope names: a service, an area, an environment, or the [factory-wide settings record](../09-gate-policy/02-one-shape-across-all-of-them.md). Ten of them move as outcomes arrive, where the change, exposure and context groups are computed from the one change under decision. They are fields of a **score version**, a row of the [decision log](../../deferred.md) appended by the log when the score calls for it and chained like every row there. It names the published formula with the weight it gives each factor, the factor set, and every value the score supplies, with a supplied value for ten of the eleven rows and none for the [list of allowed predicate kinds](../07-contracts/06-what-a-consumer-declares.md), which no outcome teaches. The weights are a field of the version from the first row and not a fact kept beside it: a decision re-scored under the version it names has to return the number it was decided on, which is what the version is recorded for, and a formula recorded without its weights is a shape and not a function. Every decision names the version in force. Keeping the supplied value here rather than in the record that holds the authored one is what leaves both single-writer, and it keeps a [safeguard](../09-gate-policy/02-one-shape-across-all-of-them.md)'s floor distinguishable from the value moving inside it — one field two components wrote could not express that. The cost: a long sequence of versions, most differing in one number the decisions naming them never read, and a [value in force](../09-gate-policy/02-one-shape-across-all-of-them.md) that is a read of three things rather than one.

A version that differs in a supplied value having moved takes effect as it is appended — gating each would gate the learning itself. A version that changes the published formula or the factor set is a different event, because what an owner authors is compared against the number the published formula returns: under a changed formula the same change gets a different number, so the authored value would go on being displayed while meaning something else, and an owner who set a [risk threshold](../09-gate-policy/01-what-is-in-it.md) after an [incident](../08-operations/06-incidents.md) would hold a number the score had silently redefined under them. So such a version does not decide a gate an authored threshold binds until the owner has confirmed or re-authored that threshold against it (8), the version in force at those gates staying the prior one meanwhile — the arrangement [_A role prompt or a skill_](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/09-a-role-prompt-or-a-skill.md) already gives a version nobody approved, which simply is not in force. What it costs: two score versions deciding at once while confirmations arrive, and an owner's silence keeping an improved formula out of force at their own gates indefinitely — the direction [_The interview_](../02-intent-into-items/02-the-interview.md) already accepts for a silence that spends nothing.

A recalibration is the third thing that writes a version, and it is stated here because it falls in neither branch on its own. It is the score refitting the weights over what [_How it learns_](02-how-it-learns.md) reads, and it writes a version differing in the weights and in nothing else. It takes the second branch: under new weights the same change gets a different number, factory-wide and at once, which is the redefinition that branch exists to gate, so a recalibration is in force as appended where no owner authored the threshold and waits on the owner's confirmation (8) where one did. That decides how a recalibration over [a drifted factor](02-how-it-learns.md) ends the resolution the drift put at every gate. The resolution ends at a scope when the recalibrated version is in force there and not when it is written: at once at a scope with no authored threshold, and at an authored scope when its owner confirms or re-authors, the prior version and the resolution standing meanwhile. The version waiting is shown beside the threshold it waits on, on the screen the threshold is authored from, so the owner whose silence keeps their gates resolved is the one who sees why. What it costs is one more thing an owner's silence holds: a drifted factor stays resolved at an authored scope until its owner acts, however long ago the score recalibrated over it, and the count [_Factory_](../11-screens/01-work-ops-factory-people.md) reports per resolved factor goes on rising there for as long as it does.

A formula change is not the only thing that moves where a change lands against a fixed threshold. The per-author prior narrows as outcomes arrive, so the same change scores lower over time and an authored threshold auto-passes a different share of changes month over month with no version having changed the formula at all. That movement is not the redefinition above and is not gated like one: it is the factory earning trust, which is what a learned score is for, and what holds it to earned trust is the width rule this section already states — a prior narrowing no further than its own count of `passed` and `failed` closes supports. What separates the two is a reading rather than a rule, so the reading is recorded and published. The realized auto-pass rate at a threshold when it was authored is a field of the [policy version](../09-gate-policy/02-one-shape-across-all-of-them.md) that set it — read back as the newest version that set that threshold on that scope, which is that section's own rule — frozen there because a version stays in force while the rate moves under it, so no query over the decisions taken under it could serve as the reference. Against it sits the rate at that threshold now, and beside both the share of held-out releases that failed within each band of the number, which [_How it learns_](02-how-it-learns.md) defines and [_What the factory auto-approved, and what was undone_](../11-screens/04-what-the-factory-auto-approved-and-what-was-undone.md) reports. Read together the three say which of the two happened: a rate that rose while each band's failure share held is a score that got better, and a rate that rose with the shares rising under it is the number drifting beneath the threshold an owner set, which re-authoring that threshold answers (8). What it costs: the drift is reported and not stopped, so an owner who reads nothing keeps a threshold whose meaning moved — the same direction every lever the design hands the owner takes, and the reason the pair is on the screen the threshold is authored from.

The vector is recorded where it was computed and never recomputed. A gate firing writes it onto that firing's [_decision_](../03-gates/01-where-a-gate-is-and-what-decides-it.md) beside the number, the score version, and the [policy version](../../what-the-factory-does/02-traceability.md), so every firing produces a vector of its own. It names the per-author prior's width and the count of resolved window exits behind it, the pair being what tells a narrow prior earned on outcomes from one that only waited: a width is a claim about how much the factory knows, and a claim with no count beside it is one a human at the gate cannot weigh. Where an agent authored what is under decision, the vector also names the effort its entry ran at and the versions of the role prompt and the [skills](../10-fleet/03-what-an-agent-is-told/README.md) it worked from. Those are the two facts the prior deliberately averages over, put where a human arguing with the number can see them, and where a query can group decisions by the version in force without any of it moving the prior. It names the [input manifest](../01-one-pipeline.md) the artifact was authored from too, so the input that varies most between two runs of one model is read back with the words and the effort that were fixed.

Recomputing one later reads today's prior, today's factor set, and today's formula, not the vector the decision was made on — and the vector a human argues with has to exist while they are deciding, which is why the decision is opened at the firing rather than at the verdict.

At the four [artifact gates](../03-gates/01-where-a-gate-is-and-what-decides-it.md) the change factor is computed from the artifact under decision and at [Decomposition](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/01-decomposition.md) from the set [decomposition](../02-intent-into-items/03-decomposition/README.md) proposed, so it is weakest exactly where the decision is cheapest to get wrong. Which gate a firing is at also decides which factors are in the set at all, and that is what keeps the resolution below from swallowing the gates above a build. Exposure is read from a diff and a [build](../05-environments/01-records-and-one-long-lived-branch.md) record, and the first build of an item exists when [Implementation](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/05-implementation/README.md) finishes, so at Decomposition, Spec, Implementation plan and Tasks the exposure group is inapplicable and not unavailable — no vector there records it, and nothing there is resolved on its account. That is the same distinction [_A role prompt or a skill_](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/09-a-role-prompt-or-a-skill.md)'s row already turns on, stated here for the four rows of the default path it also governs, and for its reason. Treating a factor the gate was never going to have as missing would put a human at every one of those gates forever — a decision nobody made, arriving as a side effect. What it costs is that the four gates above a build decide on the change, author and context groups alone, and the one factor a stranger's sentence cannot talk past is not among them until there is code to read it from, which is why [_Reports_](../02-intent-into-items/01-intake/02-reports.md) resolves a human at Spec on the source instead.

A factor the score cannot compute is resolved and never valued. The vector records which factor was unavailable and why rather than leaving a gap a reader has to interpret, and a decision whose vector records one is a human's whatever the formula returns. It binds that firing and no other, a vector being computed at one firing and never recomputed, so how far a resolution reaches is decided by whether its cause still holds: a supplier still down, a factor still drifted, a toolchain still without an extractor are found again by the next firing's own vector and resolve it again on their own, and a supplier that came back is not carried forward as a resolution nothing can lift. One cause does not recur on its own and is carried instead. A [truncated read](../01-one-pipeline.md) is a property of the artifact version, not of the moment, and a stage authoring from a truncated artifact produces one that is complete in form again — so the withholding is written onto every artifact version an item's later stages author from a truncated one, and every gate below the truncation resolves too. What that costs is the direction [_One pipeline_](../01-one-pipeline.md) already names for it, one gate further: a single truncated read puts a human at every remaining gate of that item, and the way out is to re-author the artifact rather than to re-fire the gate. That covers an input the score could not read, a [build](../05-environments/01-records-and-one-long-lived-branch.md) whose diff it could not take, an evaluation that failed outright, a factor [calibration found drifted](02-how-it-learns.md), a toolchain with no extractor behind the exposure list, and an artifact version authored from [a truncated read](../01-one-pipeline.md). The reason it may not be a value: the number is compared against a threshold, so a factor that silently reads as absent reads as low risk, and the gate a failure would remove is the gate that failure is evidence for needing — and no value one factor takes reaches that threshold under every formula either, unless the formula is written to make that factor dominant, which is a constraint this section declines to impose, how far any factor moves the number being the formula's. Direction is not sufficiency, so what must not depend on a component being up is the decision and not a term in a sum. The formula still runs on the factors that were computable and the number is recorded beside the resolution rather than left out, so that calibration has the reading it would otherwise lose on every resolved decision; it is shown when every number is, beside the verdict and never while the human decides, which [_Where a gate is, and what decides it_](../03-gates/01-where-a-gate-is-and-what-decides-it.md) already requires and a resolved row needs more than any other — the number there decided nothing, and a human shown it first would read it as though it had. The resolution being a recorded fact and not a low number is also what lets the [held-out sample](02-how-it-learns.md) honour its own exclusion: it may not select past a resolved vector, which is read off the decision rather than inferred from where the number landed. What it costs: a fault in whatever supplies a factor puts humans at gates across the factory at once with no threshold an owner authors relieving it, and from [_Work_](../11-screens/01-work-ops-factory-people.md), one item at a time, that looks like the score having changed its mind rather than an outage. What tells the two apart is that the naming is read in aggregate as well as per decision: [_Factory_](../11-screens/01-work-ops-factory-people.md) reports how many gates a resolved factor put a human at, grouped by which factor, over the same span as its other numbers — so an outage in one supplier is one count rising and a score that got stricter is not, and no owner has to open decisions to find out which happened. That is what makes the naming not optional.
