# Room, and what an environment costs

What composing one takes is written, not estimated: the environment record carries when the deployer began composing it and when the run could start, written by the deployer as it does the work, so [_Factory_](../../11-screens/01-work-ops-factory-people.md) reports composition time per service beside throughput. That is a number an owner reads before deciding whether the platform is the bottleneck, and the one the throughput argument in [_An environment per candidate_](README.md) rests on. What an install has to provide is stated the same way, as a shape rather than a figure: the factory itself, which is one process and one store, and then per item in progress one environment holding that service's build, its store, and the current release of each dependency in the transitive closure. So the platform an operator provisions is linear in items in progress with the closure as the coefficient, and the bound on items in progress is the room the platform has, held at [_Deploy to candidate environment_](../../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/06-deploy-to-candidate-environment.md) when it runs out. The figure is the install's, because the closure and the release are: the design refuses to state a capacity for the reason it refuses to model one below.

How much room the platform has is read rather than modelled, on the same terms as composition time. The deployer keeps a [last check](../../08-operations/08-drift-detection.md) for the platform beside the ones it keeps per persistent target, overwritten each pass with three counts: how many candidate environments the factory's records hold as standing, how many the platform reports holding for the factory, and the room the platform reports where it reports one. [_Factory_](../../11-screens/01-work-ops-factory-people.md) shows the three beside composition time, so an owner reads how near the install is to the hold at [_Deploy to candidate environment_](../../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/06-deploy-to-candidate-environment.md) before items stop there, rather than inferring it from throughput after they have. A candidate environment the platform holds and the records mark torn down is a teardown that failed, and the deployer tears it down again on its next pass, keyed on the environment as [its restart](../../08-operations/01-the-health-monitor.md) is keyed on the deploy record and the target, so a leak does not consume the room for good. Where the platform reports no room figure, the two counts are what Factory shows and the hold at the gate stays the discovery: the factory reads what the platform reports and computes nothing over it, which is the refusal below holding. It is the one record of the deployer's that [_Drift detection_](../../08-operations/08-drift-detection.md)'s reading of last checks leaves alone, for the reason a record per candidate target was refused there: a deployer stopped composing is a stalled timeline in [Work](../../11-screens/01-work-ops-factory-people.md) already.

What an environment per candidate costs in hosting is counted rather than modelled. **Environment-hours** is composition start to teardown, summed across every compose-and-reclaim cycle an item's environment goes through, read from the timestamps the deployer already writes on the environment record at each. Where the service record's [environment-hour rate](../../08-operations/05-service-level-objectives.md) is in force at a given write, the deployer writes the converted amount for that span beside the timestamps, fixed at the write and never repriced later. [_Factory_](../../11-screens/01-work-ops-factory-people.md) reports it beside composition time, per service and per item. The refusal this paragraph held is overturned: it argued a bound would need the factory to know what an environment costs on the hosting the owner pays for outside the factory, but environment-hours is a recorded fact of a record that already exists and not a model of what capacity costs. What an owner may bound is authored at [_Deploy to candidate environment_](../../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/06-deploy-to-candidate-environment.md), beside the platform's own room.
