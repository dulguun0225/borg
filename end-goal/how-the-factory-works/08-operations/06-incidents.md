# Incidents

An incident is a record on a production environment, written by the health monitor at the crossing against the release running there, whether or not that release's window is still open. It names which of the three readings crossed — the comparison against the [control](01-the-health-monitor.md), the reading against the service's own recent history, or a threshold an owner's [safeguard](../09-gate-policy/02-one-shape-across-all-of-them.md) set. It also names the boundary that reading was crossed against: its size, and the confidence or the [average run length](01-the-health-monitor.md) in force, beside the [policy version](../../what-the-factory-does/02-traceability.md) and [score version](../04-risk-score/01-factors-at-least.md) at the reading. That is the same reason [the window](02-the-analysis-window.md) records its own parameters at the open: a crossing is not interpretable against anything but the boundary it was actually read against — and it is what makes the rate each reading fires at on a service that is fine countable after the fact rather than only authored in advance. The health monitor is the only component that reads production behaviour and knows which release and which deploy that behaviour belongs to, which is exactly what an incident points at. A human-written one would put a judgment where the design keeps arithmetic, and a human's judgment about live software already reaches production through the undo of a shipped change (10) and the page they may fire.

At the same crossing, the health monitor copies the [failure records](01-the-health-monitor.md) for that service, release, and target onto the incident, a field of it rather than a link to the store. It is kept for the incident's life and is removable the way any kept record's text is, by [a redaction](../02-intent-into-items/01-intake/02-reports.md), which destroys words and never the link. What it copies is bounded by the cap already applied where they are kept, so an incident never holds more of them than the store did at the crossing.

A human's judgment about the incident's cause has a place too and it is not the incident: a rollback [marked as not caused by the release](03-overlapping-windows.md) is a record beside it, and the incident stands, because production was worse whatever made it so. It points at the deploy, the deploy at the release and at [the schema change it applied](../06-releases/05-the-deploy-record/README.md) where it applied one, the release at its item and its intent — so what caused an incident is answered by following those links, a change to the store included, the same links the release record answers from the other end. Its status advances to resolved when the crossing has stopped against what is running *and* what was raised from it has finished: a rollback stops the crossing and does not resolve the incident, production being still worse, which is what the hold and the page both say.

It is also what the health monitor keys its own deduplication on, before it calls [intake](../02-intent-into-items/01-intake/README.md): an open incident on this service and this release makes a further crossing an observation on it and never a second intent. What that costs: an incident points at the deploy that was running when it appeared and not always at the one that caused it — a consumer broken by its producer gets an incident on its own innocent deploy, and the item raised from it is what finds the producer.

The factory works the item it raised under the [attempt limit](../03-gates/05-the-attempt-limit.md) like any other. An incident whose crossing has not stopped, with no open window, [pages](07-pages.md) whoever holds (2) while the item it raised is being worked — raising that item does not answer the page. The failure records the incident carries are material for that work, reached the way any other material is: named on the [input manifest](../01-one-pipeline.md) before the stage's agent starts. Exceeding that limit turns it into an escalation (12), the same Work row a stuck feature produces: a bug the factory cannot fix is not a different kind of stuck.
