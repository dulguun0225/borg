# What is in it

The eleven parameters, each with what it limits and what a bad value does at either end.

| Parameter | What it limits | What goes wrong at each end |
|---|---|---|
| **[risk threshold](../04-risk-score/README.md)** | where the score stops auto-passing and puts a human at the [gate](../03-gates/01-where-a-gate-is-and-what-decides-it.md) | gate more and the human work the factory exists to remove comes back; gate less and a change reaches production with nobody having watched it run |
| **[the exposure bound](../04-risk-score/01-factors-at-least.md)** | where the exposure factor stops being weighed and puts a human at Implementation instead | low turns most changes into a human review at Implementation; high lets an exposure the score would have flagged reach production with nobody having looked |
| **[the advisory severity](../02-intent-into-items/01-intake/03-advisories.md)** | the bound at or above which a matching advisory rejects at [Implementation](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/05-implementation/README.md) and holds at [Deploy to production](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/08-deploy-to-production.md) | low rejects and holds on advisories too minor to matter, adding churn for little safety; high lets a severe match merge and deploy with nothing stopping it |
| **[attempt limit](../03-gates/05-the-attempt-limit.md)** | how many times a [stage](../01-one-pipeline.md) is retried, how many rounds [_the interview_](../02-intent-into-items/02-the-interview.md) asks, and how many times [_decomposition_](../02-intent-into-items/03-decomposition/README.md) runs again on a rejected set, before the [item](../01-one-pipeline.md) or the [intent](../02-intent-into-items/01-intake/README.md) appears in [_Work_](../11-screens/01-work-ops-factory-people.md) as an escalation (12) | low turns solvable work into human work and stops an interview that was about to converge; high spends agent time before anyone sees the item |
| **[item-size target](../02-intent-into-items/03-decomposition/README.md)** | how large an item is meant to be, above the minimum that it ships by itself, authored in the count of its intent's requirements an item answers — the unit [_Decomposition_](../02-intent-into-items/03-decomposition/README.md) sets | too coarse shows as attempt-limit escalations (12), with everything spent on the item thrown away; too fine shows as cost per feature and rework rate |
| **[the list of allowed predicate kinds](../07-contracts/06-what-a-consumer-declares.md)** | what kinds of assertion a consumer contract may draw from | narrow leaves assumptions undeclared, discovered only once a producer has broken one; wide admits an assertion that cannot be decided against one observed exchange, and enforcement stops being mechanical |
| **[the analysis window's size, confidence, and power](../08-operations/02-the-analysis-window.md)** | the smallest regression the comparison must rule out to close [passed](../08-operations/02-the-analysis-window.md), one value per quantity since a detectable change in an error rate and one in a latency quantile are not one number, how sure it must be before [rolling a release back](../06-releases/06-rollback.md), and how reliably a regression of that size is caught rather than reaching passed | coarser detects less and a low power lets a regression of that size reach passed routinely, ending the only watch on it; finer keeps every release under watch longer, and a power the service's traffic cannot support at the size in force takes passed away from that service's windows, which then run to the cap and buy none of what an early close buys. The traffic a comparison needs scales as the inverse square of the size and far more slowly in the confidence and the power |
| **[the analysis window's cap](../08-operations/02-the-analysis-window.md)** | the elapsed time that ends a window which will never reach its volume | short closes more windows unresolved; long holds the next deploy behind a window a quiet service was never going to resolve |
| **[window limit](../08-operations/03-overlapping-windows.md)** | how many analysis windows one service may hold open at once | 1 is the serial factory; each increment adds throughput on a high-frequency service and increases how many releases one rollback undoes |
| **[held-out sample rate](../04-risk-score/02-how-it-learns.md)** | how often the score auto-passes a change it would have gated, to keep unbiased signal on the authors and [areas](../02-intent-into-items/03-decomposition/02-what-an-item-names.md) it has stopped trusting | low produces almost no usable outcome, so the risk threshold can fall on what goes wrong and never rise; high sends changes the score wanted gated to production with nobody at the gate |
| **[review sample rate](../03-gates/01-where-a-gate-is-and-what-decides-it.md)** | how often a change the score would have auto-passed is put in front of a [duty](../../what-humans-do.md)'s human anyway, which is the only kind of row where what that human undoes and what the factory undoes are counted over the same changes | low leaves a duty reading what the [fleet](../10-fleet/README.md) failed at and nothing else, on practice that shrinks as the factory improves, with an approve and undo pair comparable with nothing; high spends human time on changes the factory was right about, which is the work the factory exists to remove |
