# A legal hold

A **legal hold** suspends [_Retention_](02-retention.md)'s removals. It is a record with one writer, [Factory](../../11-screens/01-work-ops-factory-people.md), the same writer a [safeguard](../02-one-shape-across-all-of-them.md) and a [halt](../04-stopping-the-factory.md) already have, naming a subject and a reason. While it stands, truncation and [report expiry](../../02-intent-into-items/01-intake/02-reports.md) are refused wherever it reaches, and so is deleting the [_People_](../../11-screens/01-work-ops-factory-people.md) mapping of any actor key a record within its reach names: what a hold over an approval preserves is who approved, and the mapping is the only thing that says so. People refuses the deletion and records the refusal, so no erasure-list row is written and no restore is needed to undo it. It ends only at a gate row of its own, held by a human always and routed away from the human who wrote it, the treatment [_A safeguard's withdrawal_](../../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/10-a-safeguards-withdrawal.md) already gets, for the same reason: ending a hold removes a protection and is decided rather than merely written. A [redaction](../../02-intent-into-items/01-intake/02-reports.md) inside a hold's reach is refused while the hold stands, and the refusal is recorded. That conflict, a hold standing against an erasure the owner owes, is counsel's to resolve and not this document's.
