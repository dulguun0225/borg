# Authored, and not among the eleven

A [_strategy_](../../03-gates/02-the-rollout-strategy.md) default is a field on production's environment record beside gate policy. Twelve more are fields on the service record, beside the window limit and the analysis window's parameters. Five of them are the explicit health threshold, the [service level objective](../../08-operations/05-service-level-objectives.md), the [fraction of its instances a release keeps](../../08-operations/03-overlapping-windows.md) while a rollback could return to it, the service's **maximum concurrent kept fleets**, bounding how many such fleets it may hold at once, and the [size and average run length](../../08-operations/01-the-health-monitor.md) of the reading against a service's own recent history. Three more are the [hours within which the service pages](../../08-operations/07-pages.md), the rate a [rollback proof test](../../08-operations/03-overlapping-windows.md) runs at, and the periods of a [change freeze](../04-stopping-the-factory.md). The last four are the [backlog cap](../../08-operations/03-overlapping-windows.md), the [search budget](../../08-operations/03-overlapping-windows.md), the [bake volume](../../03-gates/02-the-rollout-strategy.md), and the [mutation floor](../../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/07-merge-to-master.md).

Each is supplied where nothing is authored, and a safeguard (9) moves each one way only: the bake volume and the mutation floor it may raise and never lower, the rest it may lower and never raise. A safeguard on the strategy default or the threshold is the owner's duty (9). Six are authored outright instead, with nothing supplied: the objective, the fraction, the maximum concurrent kept fleets, the hours, the proof test rate, and the freeze. The first five leave a safeguard nothing to constrain, and a safeguard (9) on the sixth may add a period or lengthen one and may never shorten one. The fraction's default is all of them, fixed rather than supplied, because the score does not model capacity. The hours' default is every hour, because nothing the factory observes says which service may wait for morning, and no proof test runs at all where an owner authors no rate. A [_spend ceiling_](../../10-fleet/08-a-spend-ceiling.md) is a limit on what the factory spends through one credential, reaching which is a [hold](../../03-gates/04-what-a-gate-may-change.md). It is a field of the [_People_](../../11-screens/01-work-ops-factory-people.md) declaration that records who lent the credential, and is authored outright for the same reason: its evidence is cost, which the score does not learn from, so nothing supplies a value and there is nothing for a safeguard to constrain. A [maximum concurrent candidate environments](../../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/06-deploy-to-candidate-environment.md), a field of the factory-wide settings record, is authored outright on the same ground. A service at its kept-fleets cap stops deploying rather than losing a recovery a window could still call for, and [_Ops_](../../11-screens/01-work-ops-factory-people.md) shows the stop. None of these is authored here — each is authored and is not among the eleven, like [_Retention_](02-retention.md).

An area's [hazard severity](../../02-intent-into-items/03-decomposition/03-hazard-severity.md) is authored outright as well, on the area record beside the item-size target. It is authored for the reason the objective is: nothing the factory observes says what harm the software can do. No safeguard constrains it either, there being no supplied value to clamp, and the [area chain](../../02-intent-into-items/03-decomposition/02-what-an-item-names.md) already does what one would, the value in force being the highest named anywhere on that chain.

The [mutant cap](../../05-environments/02-an-environment-per-candidate/README.md) is authored the same way: a fixed default on the service record, leaving a safeguard nothing to constrain. So is the cap on a release's distinct [failure record](../../08-operations/01-the-health-monitor.md) keys, except that a safeguard (9) may lower it and never raise it. So is the bound above which a criterion is [unreliable](../../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/02-spec/02-in-force-and-withdrawal.md), except that a safeguard (9) may raise it and never lower it, lowering being what takes a criterion out of the gate.
