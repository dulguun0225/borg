# What the factory auto-approved, and what was undone

The Factory screen reports two rates side by side: how much the factory auto-approved, and how often a human later undid it (10) or the factory rolled it back. How much autonomy to allow is decided from the pair, and every outcome the second of them counts is one the [risk score](../04-risk-score/README.md) learns from. The human's load reported beside them is what tells an owner which side of the pair they are on. An auto-approval rate of four in five reads one way when the fifth row waited an hour and another when it waited a week, and without the wait the rate is compatible with an owner who is the factory's bottleneck and cannot tell. The [risk threshold](../09-gate-policy/01-what-is-in-it.md) is authored against the pair and this number together — the score [refuses to loosen it on cost](../04-risk-score/02-how-it-learns.md) because the owner authors what human review is worth, and a wait per duty is the number they author it with.

Two more numbers sit beside the pair, because the pair says how much the factory auto-approved and never whether the number it auto-approved on ranks anything. That number is the one the [risk score](../04-risk-score/README.md)'s formula returns and the one a threshold is compared against. The two are the realized auto-pass rate at each threshold in force against the rate [recorded on the policy version that set it](../09-gate-policy/02-one-shape-across-all-of-them.md), and the share of [held-out](../04-risk-score/02-how-it-learns.md) releases whose windows failed within each band of that number, each band with the count of resolved held-out windows behind it. Both are shown per [factor set](../04-risk-score/01-factors-at-least.md), the scale the threshold is read on being fitted per set. Read together they separate a score that got better from a score whose meaning moved under the threshold an owner set — the distinction [_Factors, at least_](../04-risk-score/01-factors-at-least.md) draws and this screen is where it is read, the threshold being authored here.

The same pair per human, beside how long each row was open in front of them, is what says whether the review is worth anything, and it is reported split by what put the human at the row. Beside the pair sit the rows each human [referred](../03-gates/01-where-a-gate-is-and-what-decides-it.md) and the rows each rejected, the rejections split the same way and by how each resolved, on the terms [_How it learns_](../04-risk-score/02-how-it-learns.md) reads them: the re-authored version approved having changed what the rejection named, approved unchanged, rejected again, or the item reaching the attempt limit. A human whose rejections are mostly approved unchanged is rejecting at no cost to themselves and moving the threshold by it, and this is where that is read. Only one of those kinds compares. A [review-sampled](../03-gates/01-where-a-gate-is-and-what-decides-it.md) row is a change the factory itself would have auto-passed, where a threshold sent a human what the score put above the line and the auto-pass rate is over what fell below it. A human whose undo rate on the sampled rows matches the factory's, in the time it takes to open a row and close it, is a signature at that gate and not a check, and [_Where a gate is, and what decides it_](../03-gates/01-where-a-gate-is-and-what-decides-it.md) says what that reading is for and what it is not. How many such rows a duty gets is the [review sample rate](../09-gate-policy/01-what-is-in-it.md) an owner authors (8), so a rate authored at zero buys the human time back and leaves this screen nothing to say about that duty.
