# Deploy to candidate environment

The dependency its hold waits on was declared at decomposition, so what this gate waits for is mostly known before the item was ever built. Here the wait is for that dependency to become live at all, since the environment is composed from it. The same dependency is checked once more at the production deploy gate, where the question is whether it is live still. One condition here decomposition could not have declared: a platform with no room for another environment, which holds rather than rejects. A rejection would count an attempt and teach the score something the item did not do, so instead it holds and lifts when an environment is freed: an item merging, dropped, or superseded, or one [reclaimed](../../05-environments/02-an-environment-per-candidate/README.md) from an item running nothing. What that costs: an item stopped for a reason no artifact of it explains and no parameter of an owner's limits — the factory's own infrastructure ceiling, visible only as a wait. A **maximum concurrent candidate environments** is authored outright on the [factory-wide settings record](../../09-gate-policy/02-one-shape-across-all-of-them.md), nothing supplied and no safeguard narrowing it, on the ground [_What is not in it_](../../09-gate-policy/03-what-is-not-in-it/README.md) gives the spend ceiling: the factory has no outcome to learn either from. It holds here beside the platform's own room, whichever of the two is reached first, and is an authored count rather than a figure the platform reports.

A second condition decomposition could not have declared is a service its owner has not yet marked [provisioned](../../02-intent-into-items/03-decomposition/README.md) on its record: a repository nobody created, or a store missing from a persistent environment. It holds here as the dependency does and lifts when the owner writes the field. An item on such a service was already stopped at [dispatch](../../02-intent-into-items/05-dispatch.md) of its implementation stage, so the field is read again here for the reason every hold of this kind is read at the moment of firing: what was true at dispatch is not what is true now.

Nothing else attaches to this row: no strategy, no rollout, no analysis window, for the reason [_What the candidate environment decides_](../../05-environments/04-what-the-candidate-environment-decides/README.md) gives. What the deploy provides is the criteria.
