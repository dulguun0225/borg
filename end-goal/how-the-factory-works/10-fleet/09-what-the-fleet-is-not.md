# What the fleet is not

**It is not a [duty](../../what-humans-do.md).** Supplying a credential is not work done with the factory — it is the substrate the factory runs on, like the host the owner already provides, and it stays outside the twelve for the same reason installing [_Drift detection_](../08-operations/08-drift-detection.md) does. Routing needs a named human rather than a duty number: the row reaches whoever [_People_](../11-screens/01-work-ops-factory-people.md) records as having lent the credential, and the owner where that person is the one who took it back or where nobody has lent one yet, which is where a [_page_](../08-operations/07-pages.md) already widens to. The drawback: nothing scores the obligation — the availability [_A credential taken back_](06-a-credential-taken-back.md) describes depends on goodwill, and no duty and no parameter reaches it.

**It is not what an agent is told.** A [role prompt](03-what-an-agent-is-told/README.md) and a [skill](03-what-an-agent-is-told/README.md) are the factory's, authored by an agent — or entered unauthored from what shipped, the exception [_What an agent is told_](03-what-an-agent-is-told/README.md) states, install's entries alone ungated — written by the artifact store, and decided at a gate; the entry is the owner's, written by hand and decided by nobody. The division is the record: what an agent runs on is substrate, and the factory reads substrate and never writes it, where what an agent is told is a record the factory has to be the only writer of. What it costs: the two are set on one screen and by two different people, so an owner who changes a model and a factory that revises a role prompt in the same week have between them changed how an agent behaves twice, and no record joins the two changes.

**It is not gate policy.** A credential reference is a field on the fleet entry the way a [strategy](../03-gates/02-the-rollout-strategy.md) default is a field on production's environment record, and [_Gate policy_](../09-gate-policy/03-what-is-not-in-it/README.md) stays at its eleven rows. Who lent which credential is a People declaration, enforced by nothing, like the duties a [_page_](../08-operations/07-pages.md) routes on; the [spend ceiling](08-a-spend-ceiling.md) authored beside it is the one field on that declaration the factory enforces.

**It is not what limits spend.** Two limits reach what an agent spends — [_the attempt limit_](../03-gates/05-the-attempt-limit.md), which counts attempts and is gate policy, and [_A spend ceiling_](08-a-spend-ceiling.md), which counts units and is not — and the entry holds neither: the ceiling is authored on the People declaration beside who lent the credential, and the entry names the credential and says nothing about what may be spent through it.
