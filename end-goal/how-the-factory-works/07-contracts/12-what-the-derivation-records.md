# What the derivation records

A [consumer contract](06-what-a-consumer-declares.md) is a derivation, and [_What a consumer declares_](06-what-a-consumer-declares.md) says what one holds and where its authority comes from. This is what the record carries about the derivation itself: which extractor produced it, what that extractor could not follow, why one could not run at all, and what happens to every record in force when an upgrade ships a different extractor. Each is a field or a step that readers the document already has use, and none is a new reader.

**The record names the extractor and what it could not follow.** A consumer contract names the extractor that derived it, the toolchain and the factory version that shipped that extractor, and lists the constructs the extractor met and could not follow: a read through reflection, a string-keyed access, a generated accessor, a mapping read from configuration. Its direction is the [transition check](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/05-implementation/01-the-transition-check.md)'s. A predicate in it is a read or a write the extractor showed, and the record's silence on an element never shows that the consumer does not touch it. A record whose list is empty is complete, and one whose list is not is partial. The [deprecation list](08-deprecation.md) reads a partial record the way [_Which producer a consumer reaches_](11-which-producer-a-consumer-reaches.md) says it reads a could-not-derive record: the consumer stays on the list of every marked element of every producer contract the record names. What lifts it is the case _What a consumer declares_ leaves to the owner, a safeguard (9) on the consumer supplying predicates for the constructs the record lists; the derived and the supplied predicates together are then read as complete. Nothing else reads a partial record differently from a complete one. Its predicates reject a producer's candidate as any do, the edges it found compose the candidate environment, and the [context factor](../04-risk-score/01-factors-at-least.md) reads from the edge derivation which services consume what a candidate publishes; that derivation either answers or is a could-not-derive record. What it costs is that one reflective read anywhere in a consumer holds every removal its producers attempt until an owner has authored the predicates for it, and that the list is as good as the extractor's account of its own limits.

**A could-not-derive record names its cause.** No extractor for the toolchain, or an extraction that failed, and for the second what the extractor reported. The two call for different responses: the first is lifted by the extractor shipping, below, and the second by an item on the consumer or on the extractor. A record that cannot tell them apart leaves the reader at the gate to guess which, on a consumer the record already says nobody could read.

**An upgrade that changes an extractor derives again.** An extractor ships with the factory, so a derivation is a function of the code and of the factory version, and a record naming only the code would be right about the code and silent about the extractor. At an upgrade's first start where the shipped extractor for a toolchain changed or was added, a fact of the factory's version, the [install's first-start step](../10-fleet/07-a-fleet-proposal.md) derives a consumer contract again for every release in force on that toolchain. It derives from the build the release names, held in the [artifact store](../01-one-pipeline.md), and writes through the artifact store beside the earlier record and never over it, the new record naming the new extractor. A release's contract in force is its derivation by the newest extractor. So a consumer with no items for a year declares what its last release read as the newest extractor reads it, its code unchanged and its assumptions with it, and a could-not-derive standing for want of an extractor lifts when the extractor ships, with no item on the consumer. What it costs is the extractor run over every release in force on the toolchain at the upgrade's first start, and a producer's gate outcome moving on an upgrade with no item shipping. A removal the old extractor's reading licensed can be blocked by the new one's, and a read the old extractor invented drops, both being what a corrected extractor is for.
