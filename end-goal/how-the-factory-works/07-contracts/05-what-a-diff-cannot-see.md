# What a diff cannot see

A diff proves the shape is unchanged, not that callers still work. A field that quietly stops being populated, or that keeps its name and its type and changes its units, breaks a consumer without breaking a contract. Neither promise catches it: both are checked against the schema, and the damage is in the semantics. Two layers answer it, a third is refused below, and none of them is a recording.

**Meaning is declared, so the diff can see it.** A unit belongs to a field's identity rather than to a note about it — `send_time_millis`, not `send_time` — and so does whether the field is always populated. A change of units is then a rename, a rename is a breaking diff, and the four items of a migration handle it with nothing new. The drawback is the authoring: the factory holds a contract to the consumer contract when it is written, an interface adopted from outside the factory arrives without it, and retrofitting the name is itself a breaking change — four items to rename a field no behaviour depends on. It covers what someone thought to declare and nothing else.

**The producer profiling its own fields is refused.** The layer this was is a population rate per field, the spread of enum values, and the distribution of numbers on the producer's own traffic, each compared against the release replaced. It would catch a field that drops from populated 99.9% of the time to 80%, and a unit swapped for one a thousand times finer. It is refused because nothing provides it, and what would has been ruled out on purpose. The emission [_The health monitor_](../08-operations/01-the-health-monitor.md) ships into every service carries nothing of a request's content, and a per-field profile is content aggregated per field. The store's series grow with releases and targets and never with a schema, and a profile's keys grow with every field and every enum value a contract declares. And no component reads or writes such a series. A layer stated without an emission, a kept shape, and a writer is a claim rather than a mechanism, and this document keeps mechanisms. What the refusal costs is that no layer runs on production traffic against a semantic break: what covers one after the deploy is the consumer breaking in its own error rate and raising an item there, and a human undoing the change (10) while it can still be undone.

**A consumer's assumptions are checked against the candidate.** The factory already knows who consumes what, so the candidate running on its own environment is checked against every consumer contract, and a failure is a rejection at the Merge to master gate like any breaking diff. No [window](../08-operations/02-the-analysis-window.md) limits it — a consumer called once a month is covered exactly as well as one on a hot path. A [consumer contract's predicate](06-what-a-consumer-declares.md), one assertion the factory can decide against a single observed exchange, has a third outcome for the same reason a [criterion](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/02-spec/03-the-six-patterns.md) does: where the candidate's run produced no exchange to decide it against, it is **undecided** rather than passing. Undecided is read at the Merge to master gate the way a failure is, which is the treatment [_What the candidate environment decides_](../05-environments/04-what-the-candidate-environment-decides/01-the-third-outcome.md) already sets out. A predicate decided against nothing passes every check the factory has and assures nothing, and the consumer whose path a run never exercised is exactly the consumer the check exists for — so counting an unexercised predicate as a pass would make the coverage this paragraph claims a coverage of whatever the producer's own run happened to touch. What it costs: a consumer on a path the run cannot reach holds its producer's merge until the run reaches it, which is a stop where the alternative is a promise nothing tested. [_What a consumer declares_](06-what-a-consumer-declares.md) sets out what may be declared, where a consumer contract comes from, and what it requires.

Replaying each consumer's recorded actual usage is the layer that is not built, and not for its size. What a recording proves is limited to what ran inside its retention window, so the expensive option is also the one with the gap: a consumer on a monthly path falls outside any window worth keeping. A consumer contract gives the same protection without the recording.

What neither of the two catches is a semantic change nobody declared. What catches that is the consumer breaking in its own error rate and raising an item there, the criteria confirmed at the [Spec gate](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/02-spec/README.md), and [a human undoing the change after it shipped](../../what-humans-do.md) (10) while it can still be undone.
