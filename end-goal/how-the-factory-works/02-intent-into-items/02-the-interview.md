# The interview

The factory runs the interview and decides when it has asked enough. It asks (3) until it has enough to decompose the intent and author a spec per item whose criteria fit the six patterns [_Spec_](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/02-spec/03-the-six-patterns.md) sets out — and what ends the questioning is having enough to author, not a human declaring the intent clear. An owner cannot know what those patterns will demand of an answer; the factory is the one that has them.

What ends the interview is one more round, fixed in form: the factory states, in the requester's own terms, what it has understood is wanted and what it will build, and the requester's answer confirms that reading or corrects it. Confirmed, the intent is refined; corrected, the answer attaches like any other and the factory asks again, the correction counting a round like every round. It is owed on an intent that has a requester, which is an owner's request (1), and on an intent grouped from [_reports_](01-intake/02-reports.md) it reaches whoever holds (3), the reporter being reachable by nothing. An intent the factory raised has no requester, its evidence is the request, and it takes no such round, nobody being able to say the evidence was misread.

At that round the requester also proposes a **tier**: an ordinal value on a small fixed scale that later orders which item is admitted first. It takes [the shape every authored value has](../09-gate-policy/02-one-shape-across-all-of-them.md): a value in force and a policy-version copy — so an owner's override is a new version and not a rewrite of what the requester proposed. An intent the factory raised proposes none, taking its tier instead from [the constraint or detector that raised it](01-intake/README.md).

No gate fires on that round and no [score](../04-risk-score/README.md) reads it. The two places a human could otherwise ratify the factory's reading of a request are score-gated and in the steady state auto-pass: confirming the criteria at [_Spec_](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/02-spec/README.md) (6), and [UAT](../../what-humans-do.md) at [Merge to master](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/07-merge-to-master.md) (7). An intent grouped from [reports](01-intake/02-reports.md) is the exception, its source resolving a human to Spec whatever the number is. So without this round the requester never says whether what was asked for was understood, and a wrong thing built well surfaces after production as a new intent.

What it costs is a round on every requested intent, including the ones the factory could have decomposed without asking anything, and a wait on the requester, unbounded the way every silence at the interview is. It costs one more thing: what is confirmed is the reading in the requester's terms and never the criteria, so criteria that answer every statement of that reading and still miss what the requester meant by one are caught at Spec where a human is there, and otherwise not before production.

The reading is written as a set and not as prose. A **requirement** is a record attached to the intent, one per statement of what the factory understood is wanted, and its id is opaque, stable and never reused, minted the way a [criterion](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/02-spec/03-the-six-patterns.md) id is. Intake writes the set, called by the factory, in the same call that records the confirmation, so a corrected round writes none: the factory states a new reading, and the round that confirms that one writes it. An intent the factory raised enumerates its requirements from the evidence instead. The record carries which of three it is: confirmed by the requester, enumerated from evidence and confirmed by nobody, or derived by [decomposition](03-decomposition/README.md) as one item's share of a requirement several items answer.

A reopened interview ends at a confirming round like the first, and that round writes the new reading against the one before it. A statement the new reading restates unchanged keeps its record and its id. Every other requirement of the earlier reading is superseded in the same call, pointing at the requirements that replace it, the pointer a superseded [item](03-decomposition/README.md) already carries. The pointer is a list, since one statement may become two, and empty where the requester retracted it. The reading in force is the set the last confirming round wrote, and [_Decomposition_](03-decomposition/README.md)'s completeness check reads that set and no earlier one, so a re-decomposition is never asked to answer a statement the requester withdrew. An item stands through the re-decomposition only where every requirement it answers is still in force; one answering a superseded requirement is superseded with it, which is what keeps [_Spec_](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/02-spec/03-the-six-patterns.md)'s per-item check reading a set in force without a rule of its own. A merged item is out of reach, so a criterion in force for a release naming a superseded requirement stays as it is: it names what that release answered, and the completeness query reads through the pointer to what replaced it. What it costs is an item superseded, with its spent artifacts, for a statement the requester reworded without changing its substance, since the factory reads a statement as unchanged or as changed and nothing judges a near match. It also costs a pointer that intake writes at every reopened round and that the first reading never needs.

A requirement takes one of the six sentence patterns [_Spec_](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/02-spec/03-the-six-patterns.md)'s criteria take, EARS being a form for requirements before it is one for criteria: one statement, one response clause, so two behaviours cannot share a statement and a reading cannot be cut coarser than one response. The factory writes the reading in that form and the requester confirms it, their own words inside the frame. A statement fitting no pattern is admitted with a tagged reason and counted, the treatment Spec gives a criterion, and [Factory](../11-screens/01-work-ops-factory-people.md) reports the share so admitted beside the unwanted-condition share it already reports. What the form buys is that the two completeness checks and the [item-size target](03-decomposition/README.md) are denominated in a unit the form bounds from below, and not one the interview chooses freely. A response clause can still be broad, so the unit is a floor on granularity and not a fixed size, and the target stays the coarse bound _Decomposition_ says it is. What it costs is a requester confirming sentences in a frame rather than their own paragraph, and a round more wherever the frame reads wrong to them.

What the set buys is the direction [_Traceability_](../../what-the-factory-does/02-traceability.md) otherwise lacks. Every acceptance criterion names the requirement it answers, so which parts of a request no criterion answers is a query rather than a reconstruction, and [_Decomposition_](03-decomposition/README.md) and [_Spec_](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/02-spec/03-the-six-patterns.md) are where that query is a rejection. What it costs is that a reading has to be enumerable: a requester's paragraph becomes numbered statements, and one statement cut too coarsely hides an omission inside itself, where no check downstream can reach it.

The interview is not a stage of its own. It is the state an intent is in, which is why it has no gate and no artifact a gate decides over: intake writes the intent, the interview refines it, decomposition turns it into items. So the state is a field on the intent, and it has six values. An intent arrives **unrefined**. It is **refined** when the factory has enough to decompose it and author a spec per item, which is the same thing as ready to decompose and needs no second name. It is **re-decomposing** while a re-decomposition of a set in progress is being decided, which is what stops that intent's items while the [Decomposition](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/01-decomposition.md) firing is open.

It is **escalated** where the rounds exceeded [_the attempt limit_](../03-gates/05-the-attempt-limit.md), or where [decomposition](03-decomposition/README.md)'s re-decompositions did. That value is written rather than recomputed from either count: the limit is authored with gate policy (8) and changes, and a decision read back against a value that was not in force when it was taken is not the decision that was taken. It is **dropped** where a human ends it for good, the value an [item](03-decomposition/02-what-an-item-names.md) can already take and for the same reason: some work stops short of anything its items could show, and an end read from absence reads as nothing at all. And it is **delivered** where the person who asked confirmed that what shipped is what they asked for, which the acceptance round below is.

The state is the intent's own and runs past decomposition in five cases and no others. Exhausting either of the counts the intent keeps writes **escalated** here, the limit exceeded being the intent's own rather than any stage's. Anything sent back to the intent writes **unrefined** again, which is what reopens the interview: a [rework request](../03-gates/06-going-back-up.md) naming it, a gate's reject naming it, a replacement [constraint](01-intake/01-constraints-and-the-design-system.md)'s raise landing on it on the terms that section states, or a correction at the acceptance round. Anything sent back to decomposition writes **re-decomposing** until that Decomposition firing closes.

The state stops every component that could move an item, and not [dispatch](05-dispatch.md) alone. Three acts advance an item: dispatch putting an agent on a stage, [the gate component](../03-gates/01-where-a-gate-is-and-what-decides-it.md) firing a row, and [the merge queue](../05-environments/03-the-merge-queue.md) fast-forwarding a candidate. Each reads the state before it acts, and none of the three acts on an item whose intent is unrefined, re-decomposing, escalated, or dropped. Stopping the first alone leaves the other two: an item already at Merge to master is fired on and auto-passed by the score, and one already admitted to the queue mints a release, which is the sibling merge a re-decomposition stops the intent to prevent. An item stands at one of the three at a time, so what it waits on is one row and not three. That row is written into the log the way [a wait the factory could not compute at a firing](../03-gates/04-what-a-gate-may-change.md) is, by the component that met the state, counting no attempt and teaching the score nothing, and closed by that component when the state clears. A candidate whose fast-forward has already happened is not covered and needs none: the commit is on master, so the item is merged and past the point a rework request may be raised at all.

A human at [_Work_](../11-screens/01-work-ops-factory-people.md) ends one for good, intake writing **dropped** with Work as the caller: an escalation nobody clears, or a conformance intent whose decomposition found nothing departing. Ending an intent ends its unmerged items with it, Work calling dispatch to write dropped on each in the same action, so nothing of it waits for a second decision or holds a branch. And the acceptance round writes **delivered** where the person who asked confirms what shipped.

Everything else after decomposition is read from the items. [An intent partly delivered](04-when-an-intents-items-do-not-all-ship.md) is computed from whether its items all reached a release, and whether an intent was decomposed at all is answered by the items that exist. So the record keeps one writer: a transition is a write to the intent and goes through intake like every other, and decomposition writes items and calls intake for the one exception that is its own.

One round follows production. An **acceptance round** is asked once every item of an intent is live: what was asked for, what shipped, and the releases that carry it. An item is live on the reading [_When an intent's items do not all ship_](04-when-an-intents-items-do-not-all-ship.md) already gives it, a production deploy record naming its release and marking it complete on every production [target](../05-environments/01-records-and-one-long-lived-branch.md). It is a question record like any other, written by intake and answered at Work, and the [notifier](../08-operations/07-pages.md) delivers it by mail and chat and never a page, nothing being wrong with the software. It reaches the requester where there is one and whoever holds (3) otherwise, the routing the rounds above already use.

Confirmed, the intent is delivered. Corrected, the correction attaches as evidence, the intent goes back to unrefined, and the interview reopens the way a replacement constraint's raise reopens one; the round counts against the attempt limit like every round. An intent the factory raised takes no such round and is delivered when its last item goes live, for the reason its interview takes none: nobody can say the evidence was misread.

No gate fires on the acceptance round and no [score](../04-risk-score/README.md) reads it, for the reason the interview's last round has neither. It is the only point at which the person who asked can say the factory built the wrong thing, and a gate the score auto-passes would remove it exactly in the steady state where it is the last one left. What it costs is a wait on a requester's silence, unbounded and spending nothing, so an intent can stand undelivered for as long as nobody answers. It costs one thing more: an intent whose items did not all ship never reaches the round at all, so what is [partly delivered](04-when-an-intents-items-do-not-all-ship.md) is validated by nobody.

The interview reopens where a stage cannot author from what the intent says, which is the ambiguity no amount of re-authoring settles. A rework request names the intent, or a human at a gate rejects to it, the state goes back to unrefined, and the factory asks — a question record like any other and a round counted against the same limit, so a stage reaching for the intent every time exhausts the rounds and the intent escalates rather than asking forever. Decomposition then runs again on the answer, superseding only what it actually replaces, because an answer that moves what was asked for moves what the set should have been and leaving the old decomposition standing would ratify it against a request nobody made. Every unmerged item of that intent waits while it is open: the state is already what puts an [agent](../01-one-pipeline.md) on an intent still unrefined, and it is read here to put none on an item whose intent has gone back. Work shows each of them stopped on the intent's state and not merely as a timeline that is not advancing, which is what an item matching no [fleet entry](../10-fleet/README.md) looks like and is the one thing it must not be confused with. What that costs is one item's ambiguity stopping siblings that were fine, which is the direction worth erring in — an answer that changes what was asked for changes what all of them are building, and a sibling that shipped in the meantime is a release nobody wanted. An intent the factory raised itself has nobody who asked, so its question reaches Work with no [holder](../../what-humans-do.md) and widens to the owner, the way an unheld row does everywhere else. The sharper cost is that this is the one place work stops with nothing running and no [page](../08-operations/07-pages.md) raised — the [notifier](../08-operations/07-pages.md) still reaches the holder on the two channels that are not the page, nothing deployed being worse for an unanswered question, but no limit applies to an owner's silence, for the reason none applies to it before decomposition either, so an item can sit on one longer than any escalation would have taken.

What is written once is the statement and whatever is attached to it, which is what keeps an approval pointing at what was approved; the state advances. Nothing reads the state back to justify a decision — it says where the intent is, which is what [_Work_](../11-screens/01-work-ops-factory-people.md) answers and what puts an [agent](../01-one-pipeline.md) on the intents still unrefined.

A **question** is a record, one per question asked, attached to the intent and naming the round it was asked in, with the answer written onto it when the answer arrives. Intake writes both, called by the factory at the question and by Work at the answer, because a question is attached to the intent and takes the intent's one writer — the arrangement [_Intake_](01-intake/README.md) already makes for every write to that record, the interview being a state and not a component able to write one. Each round's own spend is on its [agent run record](../10-fleet/01-what-an-agent-runs-on.md) instead, the way every run's is. It is not an artifact: nothing gates it, no attempt is counted on it, and the spec is still what the refinement produces. What that costs is a record written twice, so a reader tells an unanswered question from an answered one by a field rather than by the record existing — and the answer is write-once, so an owner who answers badly waits to be asked again.

How many rounds have been asked is a field of the intent, written by intake at each round like every other write to it, and it is what [_the attempt limit_](../03-gates/05-the-attempt-limit.md) is compared against here. Counting the questions instead would count a round that asked three as three, and the limit counts rounds.

Questions appear in [_Work_](../11-screens/01-work-ops-factory-people.md) under the intent that raised them, and are answered there. The spec is the artifact, so there is no interview gate and none is missing: the requester confirms the reading at the last round, and approving the spec confirms the criteria authored from it (6), which is what [_Where a gate is, and what decides it_](../03-gates/01-where-a-gate-is-and-what-decides-it.md) assumes.

The drawback is that an owner cannot shorten it. An intent whose questions go unanswered does not proceed, and the end condition belonging to the factory is what makes that wait the owner's to end.

Two things go wrong with an interview, and only one of them spends anything. An owner who stops answering spends nothing — no build, no environment, no agent is running — so no limit applies to that wait and none should: a limit would turn their silence into an [escalation](../03-gates/05-the-attempt-limit.md) (12) they have to clear anyway, which is the human work the factory exists to remove. An interview that is answered and still never reaches enough to author does spend, round after round, and it has [_the attempt limit_](../03-gates/05-the-attempt-limit.md) like any other stretch of work the factory can fail at — the same parameter, authored with gate policy (8), counting rounds here rather than retries. Exceeding it appears in Work as an escalation (12), the factory saying it cannot refine this one; it still takes no gate, the spec being the artifact that ratifies the refinement. A human who answers clears the escalation and the count starts again, because the escalation is what put them there and answering it is a decision to spend more — the limit is still the only one the factory holds, and an owner who keeps clearing is choosing to spend past it, limited then by the [spend ceiling](../10-fleet/08-a-spend-ceiling.md) where one is authored on the credential the agent runs on — a stop on units spent through it, restarted by a clearing of its own and not by this one — and by the quota on the [provider account](../10-fleet/01-what-an-agent-runs-on.md) behind it otherwise. The drawback is that a limit low enough to stop an interview going in circles will also stop one that was about to converge.
