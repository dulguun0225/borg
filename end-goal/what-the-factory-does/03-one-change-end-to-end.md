# One change, end to end

The claim above is checkable only against a change followed the whole way, so here is one. An end user of the customer's product types a [report](../how-the-factory-works/02-intent-into-items/01-intake/02-reports.md) saying a screen is slow. A grouper — the component that reads the [report store](../how-the-factory-works/02-intent-into-items/01-intake/02-reports.md) and decides which reports are one problem — finds four more saying the same thing and calls [intake](../how-the-factory-works/02-intent-into-items/01-intake/README.md) once for the group; intake writes one intent whose statement summarizes them and points at all five. The intent is unrefined, so [_the interview_](../how-the-factory-works/02-intent-into-items/02-the-interview.md) asks the owner what "slow" is worth fixing to, each question a record and each round counted against [the attempt limit](../how-the-factory-works/03-gates/05-the-attempt-limit.md) (8). The answer arrives, [decomposition](../how-the-factory-works/02-intent-into-items/03-decomposition/README.md) proposes two items — one per service — and the [Decomposition gate](../how-the-factory-works/03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/01-decomposition.md) fires over the set, the score reading the [factors](../how-the-factory-works/04-risk-score/01-factors-at-least.md) that gate has — exposure being inapplicable above a build rather than unavailable, which is why the absence of one does not by itself put a human there — and putting a human at it or not.

Follow one item. [Dispatch](../how-the-factory-works/02-intent-into-items/05-dispatch.md) matches the [stage](../how-the-factory-works/01-one-pipeline.md) the item is at against a [fleet entry](../how-the-factory-works/10-fleet/README.md) and puts a model in a [role](../how-the-factory-works/01-one-pipeline.md) on it. The spec stage authors [acceptance criteria](../how-the-factory-works/03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/02-spec/03-the-six-patterns.md) in the six patterns, and the [Spec gate](../how-the-factory-works/03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/02-spec/README.md) is where a human confirms them (6). Here that is certain rather than conditional: this intent was grouped from reports, and that source is [resolved and not weighed](../how-the-factory-works/02-intent-into-items/01-intake/02-reports.md) — a human whatever the formula returns, the [risk threshold](../how-the-factory-works/09-gate-policy/01-what-is-in-it.md) an owner authored reaching it not at all. On an intent an owner raised, the same gate is the threshold's to decide. Everything below leans on one step earlier that is not score-gated: the reading of the intent the requester confirmed at [the interview's last round](../how-the-factory-works/02-intent-into-items/02-the-interview.md), which the criteria were authored from. [Plan](../how-the-factory-works/03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/03-implementation-plan.md) and [tasks](../how-the-factory-works/03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/04-tasks.md) follow, each an artifact, each a gate. Implementation authors the code, the encodings of those criteria (an encoding being the code that decides one criterion against an observed run), and the [consumer contracts](../how-the-factory-works/07-contracts/06-what-a-consumer-declares.md) the factory derives from the build; the [Implementation gate](../how-the-factory-works/03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/05-implementation/README.md) decides over the item's first build, and the score computes its change factors from that build's diff against [master](../how-the-factory-works/05-environments/01-records-and-one-long-lived-branch.md).

The candidate deploys to [an environment of its own](../how-the-factory-works/05-environments/02-an-environment-per-candidate/README.md), composed from the [current release](../how-the-factory-works/06-releases/05-the-deploy-record/README.md) of every service it depends on. The encodings run there and decide each criterion; a criterion that comes back both ways is [undecided](../how-the-factory-works/05-environments/04-what-the-candidate-environment-decides/01-the-third-outcome.md) and rejects the way a failure does, where the disagreement appeared. [The merge queue](../how-the-factory-works/05-environments/03-the-merge-queue.md) re-verifies the candidate against the master it will actually merge into, and the [Merge to master gate](../how-the-factory-works/03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/07-merge-to-master.md) reads that run — the criteria, the consumer contracts of every service consuming what this one publishes, and the [UAT](../what-humans-do.md) assignment (7) where a human holds one. The queue fast-forwards, writes the [release record](../how-the-factory-works/06-releases/02-the-release-record.md), and mints [the number](../how-the-factory-works/06-releases/04-the-release-number.md) with it.

[Deploy to production](../how-the-factory-works/03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/08-deploy-to-production.md) is the last gate and has no Reject: the number already exists, so the actions are approve and hold. The [deployer](../how-the-factory-works/08-operations/01-the-health-monitor.md) performs the deploy on the [strategy](../how-the-factory-works/03-gates/02-the-rollout-strategy.md) the score picked, starts a [control](../how-the-factory-works/08-operations/01-the-health-monitor.md) if that strategy keeps one, and writes the deploy record. The [health monitor](../how-the-factory-works/08-operations/01-the-health-monitor.md) opens an [analysis window](../how-the-factory-works/08-operations/02-the-analysis-window.md) over the deploy and compares the release against the control until the window closes at one of its four [exits](../how-the-factory-works/08-operations/02-the-analysis-window.md). Say it closes passed, the exit meaning the comparison ruled out a regression of the size worth catching: the control is torn down, the [per-author prior](../how-the-factory-works/04-risk-score/01-factors-at-least.md) of the model that wrote the change narrows, and the window's own parameters move on what it took to reach that exit.

Now read it backwards — the test. From the [incident](../how-the-factory-works/08-operations/06-incidents.md) that would have been written had the comparison crossed its [boundary](../how-the-factory-works/08-operations/02-the-analysis-window.md), to the deploy, to the release, to the item, to the intent, to the five reports one stranger's sentence started; and from any gate decision on the way, to the [policy version](../how-the-factory-works/09-gate-policy/02-one-shape-across-all-of-them.md) and score version it was decided under and the [vector of factors](../how-the-factory-works/04-risk-score/01-factors-at-least.md) the human argued with. Every step is a link that already exists because some component had to write it to do its own job. Nothing in that traversal is reconstructed, and nothing in it was written to make traceability true.
