# Surfaces

One product, four surfaces. They are split by what a human is trying to do, not by whether the data is configuration or observation — a number is only worth showing next to the control it should change.

## Work, Ops, Factory, People

- **Work** — one item is one thread. Intent, spec, plan, tasks, implementation, rollout, and the numbered release it ends in, on a single timeline, with each gate shown inline at the point where it fires. The cut is the one gate above a thread rather than on it: an intent that became four items shows one decomposition decision with four threads under it, and shows as partial where those threads did not all reach a release. Features and bugs are the same kind of item. A project is a grouping of work, not a separate place. Board and list views answer "where is it stuck" — which includes the merge queue and the windows a service has open: who holds each, what is under watch, and what waits where the windows are all open, a rollback is holding, a [_fleet entry has no credential to reach_](10-fleet.md#an-account-that-runs-out-is-a-hold), or a human is. Filtered to what is waiting on a human — pending gates, UAT assignments (7), the factory's interview questions (3), and escalations where the factory admits it is stuck (11, 12) — it is the home view and has the badge count, because answering the factory is the daily job. That filter is not a fifth surface: everything it would contain is attached to an item, and where a thing is stuck is the question Work already answers.
- **Ops** — deployed software per environment: which release of each service is running, what contracts it publishes, health, incidents, in-flight rollouts and the control each is measured against, where the strategy keeps one. Which release is running is the deploy record's answer, and where [_The reconciler_](08-operations.md#the-reconciler) disagrees with it that is what the screen shows, over the record and not beside it. An acting surface, not watch-only: exercise veto after the fact (10) — rolling back while a control is still running, raising the revert after — and fire a [_page_](08-operations.md#pages) on a human's own judgment.
- **Factory** — the machine itself. Gate and risk policy, thresholds, strategy pins, environments, the [_agent fleet_](10-fleet.md) and the credential each entry runs on — and the same screen reports the numbers: throughput, rework rate, gate rejection rate, cost per feature, how many [_reports_](02-intent-into-items.md#reports) are waiting to be grouped, what each agent is doing and how well. The ungrouped count is here and not on Work because nobody is waiting on it, and what Work filters to is what waits on a human. Cost per feature is the one number whose control is not the factory's — what limits it is the quota on the provider account each entry names, which [_The fleet_](10-fleet.md#what-the-fleet-is-not) declines to put a ceiling of its own over, and it is why the number and the entry are on one screen. Not stage definition: the stages are the factory's own.
- **People** — humans, the duties each holds, who gates what, who does UAT, and who lent a credential [_the fleet_](10-fleet.md) runs on. The duties are also what a [_page_](08-operations.md#pages) routes on, there being no rotation to declare beside them. Declared, not enforced: the model routes work today, and is the seam authentication attaches to later.

## Three properties every surface needs

**Two audiences.** Everything above serves whoever holds a duty from the owner's list, however many people that is. Duties 4 and 5 — report a bug, complain — belong to end users, who never open this product. The way in is thin and embedded in the deployed software; what they send is a [_report_](02-intent-into-items.md#reports), and it appears in Work under the intent it was grouped into rather than as a thread of its own.

**Designed for silence.** When the factory is working, there is nothing to do and the screens are empty. Empty must not be mistaken for broken: the home view at zero shows a digest of what the factory shipped, decided, and auto-approved while nobody was looking.

**Push, not poll.** Gates and escalations leave the product by mail or chat. Otherwise the factory's speed is limited by how often a human remembers to check. A [_page_](08-operations.md#pages) is the third channel and the narrow one, for a wait where production is worse until a human ends it.

## The trust number

Factory has one number that governs trust: how much the factory auto-approved and how often that was later vetoed or rolled back. Humans decide how much autonomy to allow from that number, and it is the same signal the risk score learns from.
