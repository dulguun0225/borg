# Surfaces

One product, four surfaces. They are split by what a human is trying to do, not by whether the data is configuration or observation — a number is only worth showing next to the setting it should change.

## Work, Ops, Factory, People

Four surfaces, each answering a different question, and what is on each.

- **Work** — one [item](01-one-pipeline.md) is one thread. [Intent](02-intent-into-items.md#intake), spec, plan, tasks, implementation, [rollout](08-operations.md#the-health-signal), and the [numbered release](06-releases.md#the-number) it ends in, on a single timeline, with each [gate](03-gates.md#where-a-gate-is-and-what-decides-it) shown inline at the point where it fires. [The cut](02-intent-into-items.md#the-cut) is the one gate above a thread rather than on it: an intent that became four items shows one [decomposition](03-gates.md#decomposition) decision with four threads under it, and shows as [partial](02-intent-into-items.md#a-partial-intent) where those threads did not all reach a release. Features and bugs are the same kind of item. A project is a grouping of work, not a separate place. Board and list views answer "where is it stuck" — which includes [the merge queue](05-environments.md#the-merge-queue) and the [windows](08-operations.md#the-watch-window) a service has open: who holds each, what is under watch, and what waits where the windows are all open, a [rollback](06-releases.md#rollback) is holding, a [_fleet entry has no credential to reach_](10-fleet.md#an-account-that-runs-out-is-a-hold), or a human is. Filtered to what is waiting on a human — pending gates, [UAT](../what-humans-do.md) assignments (7), the factory's [interview](02-intent-into-items.md#the-interview) questions (3), and [escalations](03-gates.md#the-attempt-bound) where the factory admits it is stuck (11, 12) — it is the home view and has the badge count, because answering the factory is the daily job. A pending gate is a [_decision_](03-gates.md#where-a-gate-is-and-what-decides-it) opened at the firing with no verdict yet, which is also what the [notifier](08-operations.md#pages) delivers and what People answers "who gates what" from. Which human a row waits on is the [duty](../what-humans-do.md) [the tree](../glossary.md) names where one is named — confirming [criteria](03-gates.md#spec) (6) at Spec, UAT (7) at the [merge gate](03-gates.md#merge-to-master) — and the owner everywhere else, which is where a [page](08-operations.md#pages) already widens when nobody is recorded; a [pin](09-gate-policy.md#one-shape-across-all-of-them) (9) adds the human and does not change who it is. What that costs is that an owner who pins a human at a gate over one area receives every one of those rows themselves and cannot delegate them without a thirteenth duty the tree refuses. That filter is not a fifth surface: everything it would contain is attached to an item, and where a thing is stuck is the question Work already answers.
- **Ops** — deployed software per [environment](05-environments.md#records-and-one-long-lived-branch): which release of each service is running, what [contracts](07-contracts.md#two-versioned-things) it publishes, health, [incidents](08-operations.md#incidents), the rollouts in progress and the [control](08-operations.md#the-health-signal) each is measured against, where the [strategy](03-gates.md#the-rollout-strategy) keeps one. Which release is running is the [deploy record](06-releases.md#the-deploy-record)'s answer, and where [_The reconciler_](08-operations.md#the-reconciler) disagrees with it that is what the screen shows, over the record and not beside it. An acting surface, not watch-only: exercise [veto after the fact](../what-humans-do.md) (10) — rolling back while a control is still running, raising the [revert](06-releases.md#rollback) after — and fire a [_page_](08-operations.md#pages) on a human's own judgment.
- **Factory** — the machine itself. [Gate and risk policy](09-gate-policy.md), thresholds, strategy pins, environments, the [_agent fleet_](10-fleet.md) and the credential each entry runs on — and the same screen reports the numbers: throughput, rework rate, gate rejection rate, cost per feature, how many [_reports_](02-intent-into-items.md#reports) are waiting to be grouped, what each agent is doing and how well. Cost per feature is the one number nothing in the factory limits — what limits it is the quota on the [provider account](10-fleet.md#what-an-agent-runs-on) behind the credential each entry names, which [_The fleet_](10-fleet.md#what-the-fleet-is-not) declines to put a ceiling of its own over, and it is why the number and the entry are on one screen. The numbers are queries computed at read time over one span the factory owns and nobody authors, the same span the digest below reports over: throughput is releases per service, gate rejection rate is rejects over firings per gate row, rework rate is items with more than one [attempt](03-gates.md#the-attempt-bound) at any stage over items reaching a release, and cost per feature is what the intent and its items spent. What one factory-owned span costs is that an owner who suspects the factory's behaviour changed reads one number computed over both halves of the change. Not stage definition: the stages are the factory's own.
- **People** — humans, the duties each holds, who gates what, who does UAT, who lent a credential [_the fleet_](10-fleet.md) runs on, and who installed [_The reconciler_](08-operations.md#the-reconciler), those last two being what routing needs where no duty owns the row. The duties are also what a [_page_](08-operations.md#pages) routes on, there being no rotation to declare beside them. Declared, not enforced: the model routes work today, and is the [seam](../deferred.md) authentication attaches to later.

## Three properties every surface needs

**Two audiences.** Everything above serves whoever holds a duty from [the owner's list](../what-humans-do.md), however many people that is. Duties 4 and 5 — report a bug, complain — belong to end users, who never open this product. The way in is thin and embedded in the deployed software; what they send is a [_report_](02-intent-into-items.md#reports), and it appears in Work under the intent it was grouped into rather than as a thread of its own.

**Designed for silence.** When the factory is working, there is nothing to do and the screens are empty. Empty must not be mistaken for broken: the home view at zero shows a digest of what the factory shipped, decided, and auto-approved while nobody was looking.

**Push, not poll.** Gates and escalations leave the product by mail or chat. Otherwise the factory's speed is limited by how often a human remembers to check. A [_page_](08-operations.md#pages) is the third channel and the narrow one, for a wait where production is worse until a human ends it.

## The trust number

Factory has one number that governs trust: how much the factory auto-approved and how often that was later vetoed or rolled back. Humans decide how much autonomy to allow from that number, and every outcome it counts is one the [risk score](04-risk-score.md) learns from.
