# Decomposition

One intent becomes one item or several — none only for the conformance intent [_Constraints and the design system_](../01-intake/01-constraints-and-the-design-system.md) raises, whose decomposition may find nothing departing, attach that finding, and write no item, no gate firing over an empty set — and an item names exactly one service. How many go to one service is a floor and not a cap: a service the work changes gets at least one item, and gets more where one item would be larger than decomposition is aiming at, or where a change cannot ship as one. Decomposition is where [_No single item may break a contract_](../../07-contracts.md#no-single-item-may-break-a-contract) is applied rather than discovered: "an item that cannot ship by itself was decomposed wrong" is a statement about this stage, and a migration with one consumer is four items over two services, three of them on the producer, which adds the new form in one item, runs the [brownout](../../07-contracts.md#deprecation) of the old one in a second, and removes it in a third — the last two the factory raises itself, the brownout when the derived list empties and the removal on the brownout's evidence, the way [_Deprecation_](../../07-contracts.md#deprecation) sets out, not written here ahead of conditions nobody can schedule. Until decomposition got a gate of its own, nothing ratified what it produced.

A service the work changes may not exist yet, and nothing about decomposition changes: one item creates it, and whatever calls it is a second item declared to wait on the first. Decomposition writes the service record in the same write as that item, because an item names one service and the record has to exist for the item's only outbound link to point at anything. Its other writer is an owner at [_Factory_](../../11-screens.md#work-ops-factory-people), putting the [window limit](../../08-operations.md#overlapping-windows) and the analysis window's parameters on it (8), and the seam between the two is the field: decomposition writes the service's identity and never a parameter, an owner writes parameters and never creates a service. A service is one repository with one long-lived branch inside it, and no repository holds two: [master](../../05-environments.md#records-and-one-long-lived-branch), [the release number](../../06-releases.md#the-release-number), and the queue's ordering are all per service, and two services sharing a repository would give it two divergent trunks, so a build of either would be made from a tree that contains the other's stale code. The drawback is that a twelve-service project is twelve repositories, and a customer arriving with a monorepo has a split to perform that nothing in the factory does for them. Master itself does not exist until the first release: the [implementation role](../../01-one-pipeline.md) commits the candidate branch with no base, and the queue's first fast-forward is what creates master. Nothing writes the service's place in an environment either — a [deploy record](../../06-releases.md#the-deploy-record) is keyed by service and environment, so a service is in an environment by having been deployed there. The item ships by itself, a service nothing calls yet being exactly the kind of thing that can. The drawback is that the factory's weakest measurement applies to the release the rest of the work waits on: a first release is [_without a control_](../../03-gates/02-the-rollout-strategy.md) whatever the score would prefer, nothing can close its window [_passed_](../../08-operations.md#the-analysis-window), where no safeguard sets a threshold (9) it is [_unmeasured altogether_](../../08-operations.md#the-health-monitor), and it has no [_rollback target_](../../06-releases.md#rollback). Each of those is stated where it arises, and this is where they arrive together. How long they last is stated too: until the second release closes a [window](../../08-operations.md#the-analysis-window) on evidence, which a service with organic traffic — requests real users are already making — reaches at its second release and a service with none does not reach at all. What the customer has meanwhile is a factory gating on the prior the product shipped for each model version rather than on a wide one, which [_A model under a name_](../../10-fleet.md#a-model-under-a-name) supplies, and no measurement of what it ships. A customer whose software is already running and already has traffic is past that state at the second release, which is why [_A service that already exists_](01-a-service-that-already-exists.md) is the ordinary way a factory starts and not the exception.
