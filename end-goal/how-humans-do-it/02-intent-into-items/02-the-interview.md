# The interview

The factory runs the interview and decides when it has asked enough. It asks (3) until it has enough to decompose the intent and author a spec per item whose criteria fit the six patterns [_Spec_](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/02-spec.md) sets out — and what ends the questioning is having enough to author, not a human declaring the intent clear. An owner cannot know what those patterns will demand of an answer; the factory is the one that has them.

What ends the interview is one more round, fixed in form: the factory states, in the requester's own terms, what it has understood is wanted and what it will build, and the requester's answer confirms that reading or corrects it. Confirmed, the intent is refined; corrected, the answer attaches like any other and the factory asks again, the correction counting a round like every round. It is owed on an intent that has a requester — an owner's request (1) — and on an intent grouped from [_reports_](01-intake/02-reports.md) it reaches whoever holds (3), the reporter being reachable by nothing; an intent the factory raised has no requester, its evidence is the request, and it takes no such round, nobody being able to say the evidence was misread. No gate fires on it and no [score](../04-risk-score/README.md) reads it: the two places a human could otherwise ratify the factory's reading of a request — confirming the criteria at [_Spec_](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/02-spec.md) (6), [UAT](../../what-humans-do.md) at [Merge to master](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/07-merge-to-master.md) (7) — are score-gated and in the steady state auto-pass, so without this round the requester never says whether what was asked for was understood, and a wrong thing built well surfaces after production as a new intent. What it costs is a round on every requested intent, including the ones the factory could have decomposed without asking anything; a wait on the requester, unbounded the way every silence at the interview is; and that what is confirmed is the reading in the requester's terms and never the criteria, so a reading confirmed and criteria that miss it is caught at Spec where a human is there, and otherwise not before production.

The interview is not a stage of its own. It is the state an intent is in, which is why it has no gate and no artifact a gate decides over: intake writes the intent, the interview refines it, decomposition turns it into items. So the state is a field on the intent, and it has five values. An intent arrives **unrefined**. It is **refined** when the factory has enough to decompose it and author a spec per item, which is the same thing as ready to decompose and needs no second name. It is **re-decomposing** while a re-decomposition of a set in progress is being decided, which is what stops that intent's items while the [Decomposition](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/01-decomposition.md) firing is open. It is **escalated** where the rounds exceeded [_the attempt limit_](../03-gates/05-the-attempt-limit.md), or where [decomposition](03-decomposition/README.md)'s re-decompositions did, and that value is written rather than recomputed from either count: the limit is authored with gate policy (8) and changes, and a decision read back against a value that was not in force when it was taken is not the decision that was taken. And it is **dropped** where a human ends it for good, the value an [item](03-decomposition/02-what-an-item-names.md) can already take and for the same reason: some work stops short of anything its items could show, and an end read from absence reads as nothing at all.

The state is the intent's own and runs past decomposition in four cases and no others: exhausting either of the counts the intent keeps writes **escalated** here, the limit exceeded being the intent's own rather than any stage's; anything sent back to the intent — a [rework request](../03-gates/06-going-back-up.md) naming it, a gate's reject naming it, or a replacement [constraint](01-intake/01-constraints-and-the-design-system.md)'s raise landing on it, on the terms that section states — writes **unrefined** again, which is what reopens the interview; anything sent back to decomposition writes **re-decomposing** until that Decomposition firing closes; and a human at [_Work_](../11-screens.md#work-ops-factory-people) ends one for good, intake writing **dropped** with Work as the caller — an escalation nobody clears, or a conformance intent whose decomposition found nothing departing. Ending an intent ends its unmerged items with it, Work calling dispatch to write dropped on each in the same action, so nothing of it waits for a second decision or holds a branch. Everything else after decomposition is read from the items — [an intent partly delivered](04-when-an-intents-items-do-not-all-ship.md) is computed from whether its items all reached a release, and whether an intent was decomposed at all is answered by the items that exist. So the record keeps one writer: a transition is a write to the intent and goes through intake like every other, and decomposition writes items and calls intake for the one exception that is its own.

The interview reopens where a stage cannot author from what the intent says, which is the ambiguity no amount of re-authoring settles. A rework request names the intent, or a human at a gate rejects to it, the state goes back to unrefined, and the factory asks — a question record like any other and a round counted against the same limit, so a stage reaching for the intent every time exhausts the rounds and the intent escalates rather than asking forever. Decomposition then runs again on the answer, superseding only what it actually replaces, because an answer that moves what was asked for moves what the set should have been and leaving the old decomposition standing would ratify it against a request nobody made. Every unmerged item of that intent waits while it is open: the state is already what puts an [agent](../01-one-pipeline.md) on an intent still unrefined, and it is read here to put none on an item whose intent has gone back. Work shows each of them stopped on the intent's state and not merely as a timeline that is not advancing, which is what an item matching no [fleet entry](../10-fleet.md) looks like and is the one thing it must not be confused with. What that costs is one item's ambiguity stopping siblings that were fine, which is the direction worth erring in — an answer that changes what was asked for changes what all of them are building, and a sibling that shipped in the meantime is a release nobody wanted. An intent the factory raised itself has nobody who asked, so its question reaches Work with no [holder](../../what-humans-do.md) and widens to the owner, the way an unheld row does everywhere else. The sharper cost is that this is the one place work stops with nothing running and no [page](../08-operations/07-pages.md) raised — the [notifier](../08-operations/07-pages.md) still reaches the holder on the two channels that are not the page, nothing deployed being worse for an unanswered question, but no limit applies to an owner's silence, for the reason none applies to it before decomposition either, so an item can sit on one longer than any escalation would have taken.

What is written once is the statement and whatever is attached to it, which is what keeps an approval pointing at what was approved; the state advances. Nothing reads the state back to justify a decision — it says where the intent is, which is what [_Work_](../11-screens.md#work-ops-factory-people) answers and what puts an [agent](../01-one-pipeline.md) on the intents still unrefined.

A **question** is a record, one per question asked, attached to the intent and naming the round it was asked in, with the answer written onto it when the answer arrives. Intake writes both, called by the factory at the question and by Work at the answer, because a question is attached to the intent and takes the intent's one writer — the arrangement [_Intake_](01-intake/README.md) already makes for every write to that record, the interview being a state and not a component able to write one. Each round also writes what it spent onto the intent, the field a stage keeps on an item, with the name of the credential it spent through. It is not an artifact: nothing gates it, no attempt is counted on it, and the spec is still what the refinement produces. What that costs is a record written twice, so a reader tells an unanswered question from an answered one by a field rather than by the record existing — and the answer is write-once, so an owner who answers badly waits to be asked again.

How many rounds have been asked is a field of the intent, written by intake at each round like every other write to it, and it is what [_the attempt limit_](../03-gates/05-the-attempt-limit.md) is compared against here. Counting the questions instead would count a round that asked three as three, and the limit counts rounds.

Questions appear in [_Work_](../11-screens.md#work-ops-factory-people) under the intent that raised them, and are answered there. The spec is the artifact, so there is no interview gate and none is missing: the requester confirms the reading at the last round, and approving the spec confirms the criteria authored from it (6), which is what [_Where a gate is, and what decides it_](../03-gates/01-where-a-gate-is-and-what-decides-it.md) assumes.

The drawback is that an owner cannot shorten it. An intent whose questions go unanswered does not proceed, and the end condition belonging to the factory is what makes that wait the owner's to end.

Two things go wrong with an interview, and only one of them spends anything. An owner who stops answering spends nothing — no build, no environment, no agent is running — so no limit applies to that wait and none should: a limit would turn their silence into an [escalation](../03-gates/05-the-attempt-limit.md) (12) they have to clear anyway, which is the human work the factory exists to remove. An interview that is answered and still never reaches enough to author does spend, round after round, and it has [_the attempt limit_](../03-gates/05-the-attempt-limit.md) like any other stretch of work the factory can fail at — the same parameter, authored with gate policy (8), counting rounds here rather than retries. Exceeding it appears in Work as an escalation (12), the factory saying it cannot refine this one; it still takes no gate, the spec being the artifact that ratifies the refinement. A human who answers clears the escalation and the count starts again, because the escalation is what put them there and answering it is a decision to spend more — the limit is still the only one the factory holds, and an owner who keeps clearing is choosing to spend past it, limited then by the [spend ceiling](../10-fleet.md#a-spend-ceiling) where one is authored on the credential the agent runs on — a stop on units spent through it, restarted by a clearing of its own and not by this one — and by the quota on the [provider account](../10-fleet.md#what-an-agent-runs-on) behind it otherwise. The drawback is that a limit low enough to stop an interview going in circles will also stop one that was about to converge.
