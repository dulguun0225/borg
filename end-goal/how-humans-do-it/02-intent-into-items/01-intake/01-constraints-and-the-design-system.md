# Constraints and the design system

Constraints (2) arrive here too and are not intents: they are never decomposed into items and never ship. One record with a reach field rather than two record types — a constraint names what it binds, and the four values it may take are the ones duty 2 sets out in [_What humans do_](../../../what-humans-do.md), where the three widest make a constraint permanent and one intent is the narrowest, a constraint arriving with a single request and binding every item decomposed from it. Intake writes it at arrival whichever [screen](../../11-screens.md#work-ops-factory-people) an owner supplied it on, because the constraint's own rules — the actor, the chain — are implemented once rather than once per entrance, and a second write withdraws it. A permanent one is withdrawn at [_Factory_](../../11-screens.md#work-ops-factory-people), which lists the constraints in force by reach, so ending one is done from that list rather than by finding the request it arrived with; one whose reach is a single intent is read and withdrawn at [_Work_](../../11-screens.md#work-ops-factory-people), on the intent it arrived with. It is never edited: correcting one is a withdrawal and a second record, so what was in force when a [stage](../../01-one-pipeline.md) was authored is reconstructible from arrival and withdrawal alone.

What the reach decides is what the factory reads when it drafts, and drafting — a stage authoring one of an item's [artifacts](../../01-one-pipeline.md) — is per item: the constraints in force over the factory, over the item's service's project, over the item's [area](../03-decomposition/02-what-an-item-names.md) or any area above it, plus whatever arrived with this intent. The interview reads less than that, and deliberately — the factory's own and the intent's own, and not the project's or the area's, because before decomposition which project the work is in is a guess and an intent's items may not share one.

A constraint's other field is its **kind**, and it decides which [gate](../../03-gates/01-where-a-gate-is-and-what-decides-it.md) can reject on one. A build the factory can diff is decided against the implementation and rejects at [_Implementation_](../../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/05-implementation.md). A licence policy is of that kind and not a document: the [build](../../05-environments.md#records-and-one-long-lived-branch) names every package it resolved and the licence each declares, so a constraint over what may be depended on is diffed against that set and rejects there. A predicate the factory can decide against an observed run is decided on [_the candidate environment_](../../05-environments.md#what-the-candidate-environment-decides) beside the [acceptance criteria](../../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/02-spec.md), and rejects at the [Merge to master](../../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/07-merge-to-master.md) gate on the same terms — rejecting on what the run shows, one the run never exercised reading as nothing, the reading and its cost stated where the run is decided. A document is decided by nobody, and what enforces it is a [safeguard](../../09-gate-policy.md#one-shape-across-all-of-them) (9) putting a human at a gate. The cost is that an owner who supplies a predicate learns of a breach one gate later than one who supplies a build, and everything spent between the two is thrown away.

A law is a constraint whose reach is the factory, and three things about a law the reach and the kind do not carry, so a constraint carries three more fields, each optional and each read by something. A constraint may name the date it binds from: drafting reads it as in force from that date and not from its arrival, and the conformance raise below runs at that date, so a regulation phased in next year is supplied now and reaches shipped services then. It may name a date it is to be reviewed by: past that date, with no withdrawal and no replacement naming it, it is a row in [_Work_](../../11-screens.md#work-ops-factory-people) for whoever holds (2), delivered by mail and chat and never a page, nothing deployed being worse for a constraint nobody re-read — and the constraint goes on binding, a review date ending nothing on its own, because a law that lapsed in the owner's diary has not lapsed. And it may bind the factory's own conduct rather than its items: a period, and what starts it. Most of what a law asks of a customer is conduct with a deadline — a breach reported within so many hours, an erasure performed within so many days — and conduct has no drafting moment for a constraint to be read at, so the constraint states the period and a trigger, and what the trigger reads is a record: an [advisory](03-advisories.md) matching a shipped build, an [incident](../../08-operations.md#incidents) on a service in the constraint's reach, or a human marking an intent at Work as meeting the trigger — the human being the only reader of a report's prose the design admits, the reports floor at [Spec](../../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/02-spec.md) already putting one there. The deadline is a field of the intent, written by intake when the trigger occurs — the trigger's own time plus the period, the time of a report's arrival and not of the mark, because the clock a law starts started then — and it is the one field in the factory computed from a calendar, the [page hours](../../08-operations.md#pages) being authored and read against one but computing nothing. What a deadline does is two things and no third. It pages whoever holds (2) for that constraint at the moment it is written, which [_Pages_](../../08-operations.md#pages) admits as the one condition outside its own — the software is no worse, the customer is, for every hour of the wait — and the page is answered when the intent ends or the human marks the obligation met. And Work shows the deadline on the intent and every item under it, so a human ordering a queue has it to order by. It moves no priority itself, gates nothing, and teaches the [score](../../04-risk-score.md) nothing: an obligation is the owner's to meet and the factory's to make visible. What it costs is a calendar read by one field, three fields most constraints leave empty, a page that reaches a human for an obligation the factory cannot itself discharge, and a trigger that is a human's mark for the case that matters most — a breach reported by an end user is a sentence a human reads, and a factory whose grouper read it for that would be the classifier [_Reports_](02-reports.md) refuses.

Drafting reads a constraint forward, and arrival is what reaches back. A permanent constraint in force whose record no intent names, with shipped services inside its reach, is a [detector](README.md)'s condition: a detector reads the constraints in force on a pass of its own, the way the [health monitor](../../08-operations.md#the-health-monitor) reads a service's numbers, and calls intake to raise one intent where the condition holds — the evidence is the constraint record, which keys the raise like every detector's, and the condition stands until the intent exists, so a pass that stopped half-done raises nothing twice and finishes on the next one. The condition is deliberately stricter than the unfinished-intent rule [_Intake_](README.md) states for a repeating detector: an intent that ended — delivered, partly delivered, or dropped — is not raised over again, a second raise being the factory reopening work a human ended, and the residue is stated rather than looped on: what a dropped conformance intent left out of conformance stays so until the owner asks again or withdraws the constraint. The constraint is the intent's evidence, so the interview and every stage hold it through that link rather than through the project's list [_The interview_](../02-the-interview.md) does not read. Decomposition is what enumerates the reach: an item goes to each service inside it that the work changes, and a service already conforming gets none, which is the floor decomposition already sets. It may also find nothing departing at all — this intent alone can ask for a state that already holds — and then it yields no items: the finding attaches to the intent, which waits at [_Work_](../../11-screens.md#work-ops-factory-people) until a human ends it as dropped, the end [_The interview_](../02-the-interview.md) gives an intent, deciding nothing was outstanding. What an item can be checked against is what the kind already decides: a build is diffed, a predicate is decided against a run, and a document's items end at a safeguard (9) putting a human at a gate, like the rest of that kind. Withdrawal ends the condition with the record. A replacement names the record it replaces — the pair is one write — and where that record's intent has not finished, the replacement's raise attaches its evidence and sends the intent back the way a [rework request](../../03-gates/06-going-back-up.md) naming it does: unrefined again, the interview reopened, decomposition run again against what is now in force and superseding what it replaces — an intent that is [escalated](../../03-gates/05-the-attempt-limit.md) or re-decomposing takes only the attachment, an escalation staying a human's to clear and an open Decomposition firing closing first, the send-back landing then — so conformance work in progress is re-aimed at the constraint in force rather than finished against one withdrawn, and never doubled. The narrowest reach needs no raise: a constraint arriving with one intent binds what is decomposed from it, and nothing shipped precedes it. What the raise costs is work the owner did not ask for item by item — a factory-wide constraint on a mature install decomposes into conformance items across every service that departs from it, each holding an environment like any other item and ordinary in everything after: interviewed, decomposed, gated, counted in [cost per feature](../../11-screens.md#work-ops-factory-people). And the raise buys enumeration, not proof: an item of that work whose kind nothing mechanical could decide — a document, a predicate its run never exercised — ships on whatever human its gates held, so an intent delivered means the reach was worked through and not that the constraint is met, and a departure that survives is found the way any shipped defect is. Without it a constraint binds forward only, and every service already in production stays out of conformance indefinitely, its criteria unchanged and still passing, with nothing anywhere saying which services are outstanding.

A design system is not a record of its own: it is a constraint whose reach is one project and whose subject is what every screen of the product is built from, and its kind decides everything below. It is the permanent constraint a project with a user interface always has, and it is chosen as much as supplied: the project uses one the factory ships with or one an owner uploads. One the factory ships with is content of the product rather than a record — picking it writes a constraint naming it, the way a [fleet entry](../../10-fleet.md) names a credential the factory does not hold, and it moves only when the owner upgrades the factory. Moving is a withdrawal and a second record, never an edit under the standing name, and each mover writes its own pair: an owner uploading over the one in force writes it in the upload, and an upgrade that changed the shipped one is detected by the factory at its first start on the new version, which calls intake to write the pair itself — itself, because an upgrade is applied by whoever hosts the install and opens no screen, and a record standing unchanged while the content it names is replaced is exactly the break in reconstructibility that correcting by a second record exists to prevent. The second record is a permanent arrival like any other, so the raise above runs against it and the services whose screens depart from the new version get their conformance items: the migration set the dependency with the widest fan-out in a product with screens needs, the way [_No single item may break a contract_](../../07-contracts.md#no-single-item-may-break-a-contract) makes a contract migration several items. What is uploaded decides what can ever be checked against it, and a design system spans the three kinds rather than taking one. As code — tokens and components with a build — it is diffable, and what the diff decides is bounded the way [_What a diff cannot see_](../../07-contracts.md#what-a-diff-cannot-see) bounds a contract's: that a screen is built from the system's components, styled from its tokens, and calls no component against its declared interface. It does not decide that they were used correctly — a dialog nested inside a dialog, a destructive variant on Save, two tokens paired unreadably are all made of the right parts — and the rules for using them, the third thing [_What humans do_](../../../what-humans-do.md) says a design system is, are a document however the rest arrived: decided by nobody, enforced by a safeguard (9) putting a human at the gate, a residue stated rather than promised away. Between the two runs the predicate kind above, and a screen is where it earns its keep: a contrast floor over the colours a render actually resolves, a name on every control, a focus order, a target size are each decidable against a rendered run of the [candidate](../../06-releases.md#what-a-build-is-called-and-when) and reject at the [Merge to master](../../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/07-merge-to-master.md) gate like any predicate. That is also the part of an accessibility regulation a constraint can carry as something a machine decides; the rest of such a constraint is a document with the [compliance officer](../../../what-humans-do.md)'s safeguard, like any rule a machine cannot read. As a document entire — an export, a page of rules — there is no build and no predicate, nothing checks the result, and the safeguard is the whole of the enforcement. Nothing about that is particular to design: no permanent constraint is checked mechanically unless it arrives as something a machine can read, which is why the compliance officer holding (2) over a regulated area holds a safeguard with it.

The kind decides what may reject on a constraint once it is inside an artifact, and nothing decides whether it reached one. A constraint is read when a stage drafts and is not itself a criterion, so a [spec](../../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/02-spec.md) authored without it has nothing a check can name as missing: its criteria fit the six patterns, the set is complete on its own terms, and what the owner asked for is simply absent. The two kinds a machine can read do not help here either — they reject an implementation or a run, and there is no implementation yet. What catches it is the human confirming the criteria (6) at Spec, holding the request beside what was drafted from it, and in the steady state the [score](../../04-risk-score.md) auto-passes that gate and nobody holds anything. An owner who wants the check has the answer the design gives everywhere: a safeguard (9). The residue is that a safeguard is drawn on an area and stands until it is removed, where the constraint it was placed for arrived with one intent — so buying the check for one request puts a human in front of every item in that area until someone takes them out.

So a designer's say continues after the gate they decided at, but unevenly. The screen's state machine, confirmed at [_Spec_](../../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/02-spec.md) (6), is enforced at [_Implementation_](../../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/05-implementation.md); what the screen looks like is checked mechanically as far as the kinds reach — provenance and tokens by the diff where the design system came as code, rendered properties by the predicates supplied — and past that by nobody without a safeguard. The cheap kind requires a human at a safeguarded gate for as long as the safeguard lasts; the enforceable ones require a designer to produce code, or predicates a run can decide.
