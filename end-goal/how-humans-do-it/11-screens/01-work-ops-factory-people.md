# Work, Ops, Factory, People

Four screens, each answering a different question, and what is on each.

- **Work** — one [item](../01-one-pipeline.md) is one timeline. [Intent](../02-intent-into-items/01-intake/README.md), [spec](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/02-spec.md), [plan](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/03-implementation-plan.md), [tasks](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/04-tasks.md), implementation, [rollout](../08-operations/01-the-health-monitor.md), and the [numbered release](../06-releases/04-the-release-number.md) it ends in, in the order they happened, with each [gate](../03-gates/01-where-a-gate-is-and-what-decides-it.md) shown inline at the point where it fires. At the [Implementation](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/05-implementation.md) gate the implementation is shown as the [build](../05-environments/01-records-and-one-long-lived-branch.md)'s diff against master, the one place the product renders code, for the reason [_What the factory does not build_](../../what-the-factory-does/04-what-the-factory-does-not-build.md) gives; at every gate the [vector](../04-risk-score/01-factors-at-least.md) is shown while a human decides and the number beside the verdict once it is written, and the [close event](../03-gates/01-where-a-gate-is-and-what-decides-it.md) records when the row was opened, both for the reason [_Where a gate is, and what decides it_](../03-gates/01-where-a-gate-is-and-what-decides-it.md) gives. [Decomposition](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/01-decomposition.md) is the one gate above a timeline rather than on it: an intent that became four items shows one decision with four timelines under it, and shows as [partly delivered](../02-intent-into-items/04-when-an-intents-items-do-not-all-ship.md) where those timelines did not all reach a release. Features and bugs are the same kind of item. A project is a grouping of work, not a separate place. Board and list views answer "where is it stuck" — which includes [the merge queue](../05-environments/03-the-merge-queue.md) and the [windows](../08-operations/02-the-analysis-window.md) a service has open: who holds each, what is under watch, and what waits where the windows are all open, an [error budget](../08-operations/05-service-level-objectives.md) is exhausted, a [rollback](../06-releases/06-rollback.md) is holding, a [_fleet entry has no credential to reach_](../10-fleet/05-an-account-that-runs-out-is-a-hold.md), or a human is. Filtered to what is waiting on a human — pending gates, [UAT](../../what-humans-do.md) assignments (7), the factory's [interview](../02-intent-into-items/02-the-interview.md) questions (3), and [escalations](../03-gates/05-the-attempt-limit.md) where the factory admits it is stuck (11, 12) — it is the home view and has the badge count, because answering the factory is the daily job. A pending gate is a [_decision_](../03-gates/01-where-a-gate-is-and-what-decides-it.md) opened at the firing with no verdict yet, which is also what the [notifier](../08-operations/07-pages.md) delivers and what People answers "who gates what" from. Which human a row waits on is the [duty](../../what-humans-do.md) the design names where one is named — confirming [criteria](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/02-spec.md) (6) at Spec, UAT (7) at the [Merge to master](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/07-merge-to-master.md) gate — and the owner everywhere else, which is where a [page](../08-operations/07-pages.md) already widens when nobody is recorded; a [safeguard](../09-gate-policy/02-one-shape-across-all-of-them.md) (9) adds the human and [names who its rows route to](../09-gate-policy/02-one-shape-across-all-of-them.md), so the check reaches the person who authored it rather than the owner by default — a compliance officer who safeguards a regulated area answers their own rows. Where the safeguard names nobody the rows go where every unheld row goes, to the owner, and no thirteenth duty is needed for either: routing needs a reachable human and not an obligation the twelve would have to score. That filter is not a fifth screen: everything it would contain is attached to an item, and where a thing is stuck is the question Work already answers.
- **Ops** — deployed software per [environment](../05-environments/01-records-and-one-long-lived-branch.md): which release of each service is running, what [contracts](../07-contracts/01-two-versioned-things.md) it publishes, health, [incidents](../08-operations/06-incidents.md), the rollouts in progress and the [control](../08-operations/01-the-health-monitor.md) each is measured against, where the [strategy](../03-gates/02-the-rollout-strategy.md) keeps one. Which release is running is the [deploy record](../06-releases/05-the-deploy-record.md)'s answer, per target where its completion per [target](../05-environments/01-records-and-one-long-lived-branch.md) differs, so that a rollout stalled on one target is visible before anything pages, and where [_Drift detection_](../08-operations/08-drift-detection.md) disagrees with it that is what the screen shows, over the record and not beside it. An acting screen, not watch-only: [undo a change after it shipped](../../what-humans-do.md) (10) — rolling back while the build it would return to is still running, raising the [revert](../06-releases/06-rollback.md) after — mark a rollback as [not caused by the release](../08-operations/03-overlapping-windows.md), and fire a [_page_](../08-operations/07-pages.md) on a human's own judgment.
- **Factory** — the machine itself. [Gate and risk policy](../09-gate-policy/README.md), thresholds, strategy safeguards, environments, the [_agent fleet_](../10-fleet/README.md) and the credential and [effort](../10-fleet/01-what-an-agent-runs-on.md) each entry runs on, the [_role prompts and skills_](../10-fleet/03-what-an-agent-is-told/README.md) with the version of each in force, and the [_fleet proposals_](../10-fleet/07-a-fleet-proposal.md) waiting on a disposition, and the [constraints](../02-intent-into-items/01-intake/01-constraints-and-the-design-system.md) in force with the three widest reaches, listed by reach — the permanent things the factory works inside, read here rather than found through the requests they arrived with, supplied here where one arrives on its own — a design system's upload among them — and where a withdrawal of one is written; a constraint that arrived with a single intent is read and withdrawn at Work, on its intent — and the same screen reports the numbers: throughput, rework rate, gate rejection rate, cost per feature, how many [_reports_](../02-intent-into-items/01-intake/02-reports.md) are waiting to be grouped, what each agent is doing and how well, and the human's own load: how many rows wait on a human now, how long a row a human decided waited, and how long it was open in front of them before the verdict, per duty and per named human — and beside the load, what each human approved and how often it was later undone (10) or rolled back, the same pair [the screen reports for the factory](04-what-the-factory-auto-approved-and-what-was-undone.md), per human. What limits cost per feature is the [_spend ceiling_](../10-fleet/08-a-spend-ceiling.md) where an owner authored one on the credential — a limit on what the factory spends through it, reaching which is a [hold](../03-gates/04-what-a-gate-may-change.md) — and the quota on the [provider account](../10-fleet/01-what-an-agent-runs-on.md) behind it otherwise, which is why the number and the entry naming the credential are on one screen. The numbers are queries computed at read time over one span the factory owns and nobody authors, the same span [the digest](02-three-properties-every-screen-needs.md) reports over: throughput is releases per service, gate rejection rate is rejects over firings per gate row, rework rate is items with more than one [attempt](../03-gates/05-the-attempt-limit.md) at any [stage](../01-one-pipeline.md) over items reaching a release, and cost per feature is what the intent and its items spent on models, per model version and never summed across them, and nothing of the substrate: not the [environment per candidate](../05-environments/02-an-environment-per-candidate.md), and not the [full fleet a release keeps](../08-operations/03-overlapping-windows.md) while a window could return to it, which is the largest cost a rollout has and lands on hosting the owner pays for outside the factory. The human's load is two reads of rows the [decision log](../../deferred.md) already holds: the rows waiting are [open events](../03-gates/01-where-a-gate-is-and-what-decides-it.md) with no close event that name a duty or a human and whose item is still at the gate that opened them — an open event an edit superseded or an escalation moved past is left open and is not a wait, the reading the decision's own section already gives it — and how long a row waited is its close event's time against its open event's, over rows a human closed, grouped by the duty or human the open event names — so no wait record the design refuses elsewhere is added, and the three holds the factory sets over a record it never writes stay uncounted. What it costs: the wait and the deciding are one interval — a row opened on Friday night and decided on Monday reads as the weekend, so the number is how long the factory stood waiting on a human and never how long a human spent. What one factory-owned span costs: an owner who suspects the factory's behaviour changed reads one number computed over both halves of the change. Two of them are the exception and have to be, because nothing else measures a [role prompt](../10-fleet/03-what-an-agent-is-told/README.md): gate rejection rate and rework rate are also readable grouped by the role prompt version in force, which the [factor vector](../04-risk-score/01-factors-at-least.md) on each decision names, so a version that made things worse is visible as two rates side by side. It is the weakest measurement on this screen — a rate needs many items to move, and a small install produces few — and [_How does the factory learn whether a role prompt version is better?_](../../open.md#how-does-the-factory-learn-whether-a-role-prompt-version-is-better) is that question. Not stage definition: the stages are the factory's own, and a role prompt and a skill are read here rather than authored, [a gate](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/09-a-role-prompt-or-a-skill.md) being where a version is decided and a [safeguard](../09-gate-policy/02-one-shape-across-all-of-them.md) (9) all an owner writes. That gate's row is decided here as well, and it is the only decision any screen holds that belongs to no item: its row names a version and no item at all, so Work — one item, one timeline — has nowhere to put it, and this screen already shows the version in force beside it. The three actions are the row's own, Approve, Reject with feedback and [Edit in place](../03-gates/04-what-a-gate-may-change.md), and reading a version here while deciding it somewhere else is what would leave the gate that reaches a human for every role prompt version, on any threshold an owner would author, with nowhere to decide it.
- **People** — humans, the duties each holds, who gates what, who does user acceptance testing (7), who lent a credential [_the fleet_](../10-fleet/README.md) runs on, and who installed [_Drift detection_](../08-operations/08-drift-detection.md), those last two being what routing needs where no duty owns the row. The duties are also what a [_page_](../08-operations/07-pages.md) routes on, there being no rotation to declare beside them. Declared, not enforced: the model routes work today, and is the [seam](../../deferred.md) authentication attaches to later. The one field here the factory does enforce is the [_spend ceiling_](../10-fleet/08-a-spend-ceiling.md) an owner may author beside a lent credential, a limit on what is spent through it that holds work when reached.
