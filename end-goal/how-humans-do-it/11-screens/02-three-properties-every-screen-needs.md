# Three properties every screen needs

**Two audiences.** Everything on [the four screens](01-work-ops-factory-people.md) serves whoever holds a duty from [the owner's list](../../what-humans-do.md), however many people that is. Duties 4 and 5 — report a bug, complain — belong to end users, who never open this product. The way in is thin and embedded in the deployed software; what they send is a [_report_](../02-intent-into-items/01-intake/02-reports.md), and it appears in Work under the intent it was grouped into rather than as a timeline of its own.

**Designed for silence.** When the factory is working, there is nothing to do and the screens are empty. Empty must not be mistaken for broken: the home view at zero shows a digest of what the factory shipped, decided, and auto-approved while nobody was looking. A digest computed from records the factory writes is empty in both cases, though — a quiet week and a factory that stopped writing produce the same page — so the home view at zero also carries the age of the newest [last check](../08-operations/08-drift-detection.md) — the record each component that runs continuously overwrites every pass — that the [health monitor](../08-operations/01-the-health-monitor.md), the [deployer](../08-operations/01-the-health-monitor.md), the [notifier](../08-operations/07-pages.md) and [_Drift detection_](../08-operations/08-drift-detection.md) have each written, and reads as working only where all four are current. What that costs: one number on an otherwise empty page that a human has to know how to read.

**Push, not poll.** Gates and escalations leave the product by mail or chat. Otherwise the factory's speed is limited by how often a human remembers to check. A [_page_](../08-operations/07-pages.md) is the third channel and the narrow one, for a wait where production is worse until a human ends it.
