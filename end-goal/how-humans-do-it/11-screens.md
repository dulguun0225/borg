# Screens

One product, four screens. They are split by what a human is trying to do, not by whether the data is configuration or observation — a number is only worth showing next to the setting it should change.

## Work, Ops, Factory, People

Four screens, each answering a different question, and what is on each.

- **Work** — one [item](01-one-pipeline.md) is one timeline. [Intent](02-intent-into-items.md#intake), spec, plan, tasks, implementation, [rollout](08-operations.md#the-health-monitor), and the [numbered release](06-releases.md#the-release-number) it ends in, in the order they happened, with each [gate](03-gates.md#where-a-gate-is-and-what-decides-it) shown inline at the point where it fires. [Decomposition](03-gates.md#decomposition) is the one gate above a timeline rather than on it: an intent that became four items shows one decision with four timelines under it, and shows as [partly delivered](02-intent-into-items.md#when-an-intents-items-do-not-all-ship) where those timelines did not all reach a release. Features and bugs are the same kind of item. A project is a grouping of work, not a separate place. Board and list views answer "where is it stuck" — which includes [the merge queue](05-environments.md#the-merge-queue) and the [windows](08-operations.md#the-watch-window) a service has open: who holds each, what is under watch, and what waits where the windows are all open, a [rollback](06-releases.md#rollback) is holding, a [_fleet entry has no credential to reach_](10-fleet.md#an-account-that-runs-out-is-a-hold), or a human is. Filtered to what is waiting on a human — pending gates, [UAT](../what-humans-do.md) assignments (7), the factory's [interview](02-intent-into-items.md#the-interview) questions (3), and [escalations](03-gates.md#the-attempt-limit) where the factory admits it is stuck (11, 12) — it is the home view and has the badge count, because answering the factory is the daily job. A pending gate is a [_decision_](03-gates.md#where-a-gate-is-and-what-decides-it) opened at the firing with no verdict yet, which is also what the [notifier](08-operations.md#pages) delivers and what People answers "who gates what" from. Which human a row waits on is the [duty](../what-humans-do.md) the design names where one is named — confirming [criteria](03-gates.md#spec) (6) at Spec, UAT (7) at the [Merge to master](03-gates.md#merge-to-master) gate — and the owner everywhere else, which is where a [page](08-operations.md#pages) already widens when nobody is recorded; a [safeguard](09-gate-policy.md#one-shape-across-all-of-them) (9) adds the human and does not change who it is. What that costs is that an owner who safeguards a gate over one area receives every one of those rows themselves and cannot delegate them without a thirteenth duty the design refuses. That filter is not a fifth screen: everything it would contain is attached to an item, and where a thing is stuck is the question Work already answers.
- **Ops** — deployed software per [environment](05-environments.md#records-and-one-long-lived-branch): which release of each service is running, what [contracts](07-contracts.md#two-versioned-things) it publishes, health, [incidents](08-operations.md#incidents), the rollouts in progress and the [control](08-operations.md#the-health-monitor) each is measured against, where the [strategy](03-gates.md#the-rollout-strategy) keeps one. Which release is running is the [deploy record](06-releases.md#the-deploy-record)'s answer, and where [_The independent checker_](08-operations.md#the-independent-checker) disagrees with it that is what the screen shows, over the record and not beside it. An acting screen, not watch-only: [undo a change after it shipped](../what-humans-do.md) (10) — rolling back while a control is still running, raising the [revert](06-releases.md#rollback) after — and fire a [_page_](08-operations.md#pages) on a human's own judgment.
- **Factory** — the machine itself. [Gate and risk policy](09-gate-policy.md), thresholds, strategy safeguards, environments, the [_agent fleet_](10-fleet.md) and the credential and [effort](10-fleet.md#what-an-agent-runs-on) each entry runs on, the [_role prompts and skills_](10-fleet.md#what-an-agent-is-told) with the version of each in force, and the [_fleet proposals_](10-fleet.md#a-fleet-proposal) waiting on a disposition — and the same screen reports the numbers: throughput, rework rate, gate rejection rate, cost per feature, how many [_reports_](02-intent-into-items.md#reports) are waiting to be grouped, what each agent is doing and how well. Cost per feature is the one number nothing in the factory limits — what limits it is the quota on the [provider account](10-fleet.md#what-an-agent-runs-on) behind the credential each entry names, which [_The fleet_](10-fleet.md#what-the-fleet-is-not) declines to put a ceiling of its own over, and it is why the number and the entry are on one screen. The numbers are queries computed at read time over one span the factory owns and nobody authors, the same span the digest below reports over: throughput is releases per service, gate rejection rate is rejects over firings per gate row, rework rate is items with more than one [attempt](03-gates.md#the-attempt-limit) at any stage over items reaching a release, and cost per feature is what the intent and its items spent. What one factory-owned span costs is that an owner who suspects the factory's behaviour changed reads one number computed over both halves of the change. Two of them are the exception and have to be, because nothing else measures a [role prompt](10-fleet.md#what-an-agent-is-told): gate rejection rate and rework rate are also readable grouped by the role prompt version in force, which the [factor vector](04-risk-score.md#factors-at-least) on each decision names, so a version that made things worse is visible as two rates side by side. It is the weakest measurement on this screen — a rate needs many items to move, and a small install produces few — and [_How does the factory learn whether a role prompt version is better?_](../open.md#how-does-the-factory-learn-whether-a-role-prompt-version-is-better) is that question. Not stage definition: the stages are the factory's own, and a role prompt and a skill are read here rather than authored, [a gate](03-gates.md#a-role-prompt-or-a-skill) being where a version is decided and a [safeguard](09-gate-policy.md#one-shape-across-all-of-them) (9) all an owner writes.
- **People** — humans, the duties each holds, who gates what, who does user acceptance testing (7), who lent a credential [_the fleet_](10-fleet.md) runs on, and who installed [_The independent checker_](08-operations.md#the-independent-checker), those last two being what routing needs where no duty owns the row. The duties are also what a [_page_](08-operations.md#pages) routes on, there being no rotation to declare beside them. Declared, not enforced: the model routes work today, and is the [seam](../deferred.md) authentication attaches to later.

## Three properties every screen needs

**Two audiences.** Everything above serves whoever holds a duty from [the owner's list](../what-humans-do.md), however many people that is. Duties 4 and 5 — report a bug, complain — belong to end users, who never open this product. The way in is thin and embedded in the deployed software; what they send is a [_report_](02-intent-into-items.md#reports), and it appears in Work under the intent it was grouped into rather than as a timeline of its own.

**Designed for silence.** When the factory is working, there is nothing to do and the screens are empty. Empty must not be mistaken for broken: the home view at zero shows a digest of what the factory shipped, decided, and auto-approved while nobody was looking.

**Push, not poll.** Gates and escalations leave the product by mail or chat. Otherwise the factory's speed is limited by how often a human remembers to check. A [_page_](08-operations.md#pages) is the third channel and the narrow one, for a wait where production is worse until a human ends it.

## What the factory auto-approved, and what was undone

The Factory screen reports two rates side by side: how much the factory auto-approved, and how often a human later undid it (10) or the factory rolled it back. How much autonomy to allow is decided from the pair, and every outcome the second of them counts is one the [risk score](04-risk-score.md) learns from.
