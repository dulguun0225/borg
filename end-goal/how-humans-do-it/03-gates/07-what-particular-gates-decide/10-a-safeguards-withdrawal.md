# A safeguard's withdrawal

The second row outside every item. What it decides is the record [_A safeguard is not edited_](../../09-gate-policy/02-one-shape-across-all-of-them.md) makes a withdrawal — a second record naming the [safeguard](../../09-gate-policy/02-one-shape-across-all-of-them.md) (9) it ends — and a withdrawal is not in force until this row approves it: the value in force reads the newest approved record for that subject, and a withdrawal nobody approved leaves the safeguard standing. It has a row because it is the one operation in the factory that removes a human from a gate, which the document refuses to any mechanism; a record that did it with no decision on it would be that mechanism with an actor field for a conscience. Its actions are Approve and Reject with feedback: there is no Edit in place, a withdrawal naming one record having nothing in it to edit, and no Hold, for the reason [_A role prompt or a skill_](09-a-role-prompt-or-a-skill.md) gives — a withdrawal not approved is already not in force.

It reads no factor set and no threshold. A human is at it always, the way a resolved factor puts one at a gate whatever the number — the [score](../../04-risk-score/README.md) deciding to remove a human is what [_How it learns_](../../04-risk-score/02-how-it-learns.md) refuses, and a row the score could auto-pass would be the refusal with a gap in it. Who that human is follows the safeguard's own routing field: the [duty](../../../what-humans-do.md) or named human its rows route to, and the owner where it names none — and never the actor on the withdrawal, so the person who ends a check is not the person who decides it ends. Where those are one person, the row still fires and the decision is still written: an install with one owner cannot separate the two, and what the row buys there is a chained [decision](../01-where-a-gate-is-and-what-decides-it.md) with an actor and a time on it rather than a second pair of eyes, which is what an auditor reads two months later. Reject leaves the safeguard in force and the feedback beside the withdrawal, and [_the attempt limit_](../05-the-attempt-limit.md) does not reach this row, there being no stage and no item. What it costs: every withdrawal waits on a human, a safeguard whose named human has left holds until the owner clears it — the widening an unheld row already gets — and the actor on both records is self-asserted until [seam 1](../../../deferred.md) carries a principal, so what the row records is who claimed to decide, still one more thing than a field flipped in place.
