# Merge to master

The release event — where a candidate becomes a numbered release, and where the verdict on the candidate is given. Approving it admits the candidate to [the merge queue](../../05-environments/03-the-merge-queue.md), which re-verifies it against the master it will actually merge into and rejects it there on the same terms if that fails; rejecting it here [sends the item back](../06-going-back-up.md) to the stage the verdict names and to Implementation where it names none, and nothing waits on the environment it used, which stays the item's until it merges, is dropped, or is superseded by a re-decomposition. The verdict is a human's when the score or a safeguard puts one there and the factory's own otherwise, and a human deciding there is performing [UAT](../../../what-humans-do.md) (7). What either reads is the candidate's own run: the acceptance criteria decided against it, every consumer contract checked against it, and the producer's own [contract diff](../../07-contracts/04-enforcement.md) — each rejecting on its own terms before anyone gives a verdict.
