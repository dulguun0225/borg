# What is in it

The eight, each with what it limits and what a bad value does at either end.

| Parameter | What it limits | What goes wrong at each end |
|---|---|---|
| **[risk threshold](../04-risk-score/README.md)** | where the score stops auto-passing and puts a human at the [gate](../03-gates/01-where-a-gate-is-and-what-decides-it.md) | gate more and the human work the factory exists to remove comes back; gate less and a change reaches production with nobody having watched it run |
| **[attempt limit](../03-gates/05-the-attempt-limit.md)** | how many times a [stage](../01-one-pipeline.md) is retried, how many rounds [_the interview_](../02-intent-into-items/02-the-interview.md) asks, and how many times [_decomposition_](../02-intent-into-items/03-decomposition/README.md) runs again on a rejected set, before the item or the [intent](../02-intent-into-items/01-intake/README.md) appears in [_Work_](../11-screens.md#work-ops-factory-people) as an escalation (12) | low turns solvable work into human work and stops an interview that was about to converge; high spends agent time before anyone sees the item |
| **[item-size target](../02-intent-into-items/03-decomposition/README.md)** | how large an item is meant to be, above the minimum that it ships by itself | too coarse shows as attempt-limit escalations (12), with everything spent on the item thrown away; too fine shows as cost per feature and rework rate |
| **[the list of allowed predicate kinds](../07-contracts/06-what-a-consumer-declares.md)** | what kinds of assertion a consumer contract may draw from | narrow leaves assumptions undeclared, discovered only once a producer has broken one; wide admits an assertion that cannot be decided against one observed exchange, and enforcement stops being mechanical |
| **[the analysis window's size and confidence](../08-operations/02-the-analysis-window.md)** | the smallest regression the comparison must rule out to close [passed](../08-operations/02-the-analysis-window.md), and how sure it must be | coarser detects less, and the traffic a comparison needs scales as the inverse square of what it must detect; finer keeps every release under watch longer |
| **[the analysis window's cap](../08-operations/02-the-analysis-window.md)** | the elapsed time that ends a window which will never reach its volume | short closes more windows unresolved; long holds the next deploy behind a window a quiet service was never going to resolve |
| **[window limit](../08-operations/03-overlapping-windows.md)** | how many analysis windows one service may hold open at once | 1 is the serial factory; each increment adds throughput on a high-frequency service and increases how many releases one rollback undoes |
| **[held-out sample rate](../04-risk-score/02-how-it-learns.md)** | how often the score auto-passes a change it would have gated, to keep unbiased signal on the authors and areas it has stopped trusting | low produces almost no usable outcome, so the risk threshold can fall on what goes wrong and never rise; high sends changes the score wanted gated to production with nobody at the gate |
