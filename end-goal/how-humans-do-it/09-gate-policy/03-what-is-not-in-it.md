# What is not in it

Three things are next to gate policy and are not it. **The score.** An owner authors what the number is compared against, never how it is computed — the formula is published so a human can disagree with it, and the [vector](../04-risk-score/README.md) behind it is learned from outcomes. **The stages.** A gate is where policy applies; what is between the gates is the factory's own — including [what an agent is told](../10-fleet.md#what-an-agent-is-told) there, which the factory writes, versions, and puts through a gate of its own, and which an owner reaches only by adding a safeguard at that gate (9). **The form a criterion takes.** The [six patterns](../03-gates/07-what-particular-gates-decide/02-spec.md) are a closed set and the factory owns the form, which is what lets it end the interview — an owner confirms criteria (6) and does not author the shape one may have.

A [_strategy_](../03-gates/02-the-rollout-strategy.md) default is a field on production's environment record beside gate policy, and the explicit health threshold, the [service level objective](../08-operations/05-service-level-objectives.md), the [fraction of its instances a release keeps](../08-operations/03-overlapping-windows.md) while a rollback could return to it, and the [hours within which the service pages](../08-operations/07-pages.md) are fields on the service record beside the window limit and the analysis window's parameters, as are the [backlog cap](../08-operations/03-overlapping-windows.md) and the [search budget](../08-operations/03-overlapping-windows.md), each supplied where nothing is authored and each lowered and never raised by a safeguard (9). A safeguard on the strategy default or the threshold is (9); the objective, the fraction, and the hours are authored outright, there being no supplied value for a safeguard to constrain — the fraction's default is all of them, fixed rather than supplied, because the score does not model capacity, and the hours' default is every hour, because nothing the factory observes says which service may wait for morning. A [_spend ceiling_](../10-fleet.md#a-spend-ceiling) — a limit on what the factory spends through one credential, reaching which is a [hold](../03-gates/04-what-a-gate-may-change.md) — is a field of the [_People_](../11-screens.md#work-ops-factory-people) declaration that records who lent the credential, and is authored outright for the same reason: its evidence is cost, which the score does not learn from, so nothing supplies a value and there is nothing for a safeguard to constrain. None of these is authored here — each is authored and is not among the eight, like retention below.

**Retention is authored and is not one of the eight.** An owner may set how long the [_report store_](../02-intent-into-items/01-intake/02-reports.md) keeps a report and how long the decision log is kept, both fields of the factory-wide settings record. Where they author neither, both are kept for the life of the install. The report channel's two rates sit beside them, authored the same way and bounding what arrives rather than what is kept; where an owner authors neither rate, arrival is unbounded, because no outcome teaches a rate and so there is nothing for the score to supply. Truncating the log is to a checkpoint and the [chain](../../deferred.md) restarts there — the truncation itself appending a chained row naming who authored the value, the value it enforced, the boundary it cut to, and the policy version and score version in force at the cut, so what was removed and under what authority stays readable, and a decision after the cut naming a version before it is still read against what it was decided under. But nothing before the checkpoint is evidence any more, and the score loses its oldest outcomes — the ones a narrow [per-author prior](../04-risk-score/01-factors-at-least.md) was built from — so an owner truncating for storage widens the priors of the authors the factory knows best, and nothing on the screen says so. The checkpoint is not a field: it is the oldest record the log still holds, and storing it beside the log would be one fact in two places able to disagree. Everything else in [the graph](../../what-the-factory-does.md#tight-integration) is kept, because deleting any of it breaks a link a record already points at — which is what one graph exchanges for the customer's own disk. Kept means the records: what is removable inside a kept record is its text, by [a redaction](../02-intent-into-items/01-intake/02-reports.md), which destroys words and never a link.
