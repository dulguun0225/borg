# Open

Nothing is open. All ten sittings of the interview are finished, and every question they raised is folded into the file that owns its subject — eleven from sitting 1, and from the nine that followed a further set the owner answered directly: that a service is one repository, that the contract machinery reaches any consumer in the factory whichever project it is in, that the decision log may be truncated to a checkpoint the chain restarts at, and that a pin may not suspend the factory's own rollback.

A question belongs here when an owner is who decides it and the tree cannot answer it by applying a pattern it already holds. It is phrased as the question and what turns on it, and it is deleted from here when it is folded.
