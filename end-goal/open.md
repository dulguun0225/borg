# Open

A question belongs here when an [owner](what-humans-do.md) decides it and the design cannot answer it by applying a pattern it already holds. It is phrased as the question and what turns on it, and is deleted from here once folded into the file that owns its subject.

## What the security predicate list ships with

[_What a consumer declares_](how-the-factory-works/07-contracts/06-what-a-consumer-declares.md) says a second list of predicate kinds exists beside the consumer contract's: the security predicates, decided per toolchain against a build, which an owner may extend and never narrow. It names no kind. What turns on it: until a kind is named, the Merge to master gate's rejection over that list can never fire, and the class of defect the [exposure factor](how-the-factory-works/04-risk-score/01-factors-at-least.md) leaves blind is covered by nothing else. It is an owner's question because each kind is a check over the code the factory writes, and no pattern the document holds says which checks those are.

## Whether already taking traffic is a fifth field the deployer writes

[_Decomposition_](how-the-factory-works/02-intent-into-items/03-decomposition/README.md) names four fields as the deployer's on the service record: a target reached, instances the platform can replace, a rollback path, and an emission the health monitor can read. [_A service that already exists_](how-the-factory-works/02-intent-into-items/03-decomposition/01-a-service-that-already-exists.md) admits one shape at adoption, instances already taking organic traffic among its conditions, and no one of the four says that traffic flows: an emission the monitor can read is not the same fact as a request rate above zero. What turns on it: whether adoption reads the rate from the emission at the moment it admits and records nothing, or the record carries a fifth field the deployer writes beside the four, which is what a reader of the record afterwards can tell. It is an owner's question because it decides what the service record says about a service the factory took over.

## Whether an owner may add a predicate kind nothing can decide

[_One shape across all of them_](how-the-factory-works/09-gate-policy/02-one-shape-across-all-of-them.md) says the list of allowed predicate kinds is one list the factory owns and an owner extends, and a safeguard on it may only extend it. [_What a consumer declares_](how-the-factory-works/07-contracts/06-what-a-consumer-declares.md) decides each kind against one observed exchange, and the security list beside it reads a kind no derivation covers as could not derive, which puts a human at the gate. What turns on it: whether extending the consumer list past the kinds the factory can decide is coverage added, resolving a human to the row for every contract naming such a kind, or the mechanical enforcement gone from that cell, which is the reading that refuses the name at authoring. It is an owner's question because each answer costs a different thing at every Merge to master row a consumer contract reaches.

## What a consumer checkout with no mirror and no entries declares

[_What a consumer declares_](how-the-factory-works/07-contracts/06-what-a-consumer-declares.md) says a service whose file names no producer for an address it reaches is could not derive until its entries are authored, the state an adopted service arrives in, and that a build not following the convention reads could not derive. A checkout holding no mirror file and no entries file reaches no address the convention can show. What turns on it: whether such a checkout is a consumer of nothing, a complete and empty declaration that lets its release ship with no human at the row, or a service that has not yet followed the convention, could not derive until an owner authors its entries. It is an owner's question because the first reading lets an adopted service that reaches producers by a path the convention does not cover ship unread, and the second holds every service that consumes nothing at a human until it says so in a file.
