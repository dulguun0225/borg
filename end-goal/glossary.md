# Glossary

A line here is for a word the wider software industry already owns that this document uses in a narrower or different sense, so a reader arriving with the industry meaning is not left confidently wrong. [`rollback`](how-the-factory-works/06-releases/06-rollback.md) is the line that matters most, because an SRE reader assumes a capability this design withholds and would otherwise rely on it. This is not an index of the document's vocabulary, and a line here does not admit a term — a term is introduced where it is first used, in the prose of the file using it. Nor is it a second place a decision is written: the line says what the term names, the section says what follows from it, and where the two could differ the section is right. That keeps it clear of `CLAUDE.md`'s rule against a list of answers kept outside the sections that own them — a resolved question is folded into its section and never restated here.

Which words have a line here is decided in the commit that adds or removes one — this file is
the only copy of that list. What puts a word in front of that decision is the check
`CLAUDE.md` runs on a changed file, where a reader given nothing else returns every term of
art this document uses in a sense other than its field's. A term that reader returns is a
failure until the prose is reworded or a line is added here.

Read it when a sentence uses a name you cannot place. Nothing depends on reading it first.

- **artifact** — what a stage authors about one item and a gate decides over: the spec, the implementation plan, the tasks, and the implementation, with the criterion encoding authored beside that last one and decided at the same gate. [One pipeline](how-the-factory-works/01-one-pipeline.md)
- **candidate** — an item plus its build: identity enough to deploy, test, and reject, from build until merge to master. It is not a record. [What a build is called, and when](how-the-factory-works/06-releases/03-what-a-build-is-called-and-when.md)
- **control** — the instances a release is compared against, running the build already in production and taking comparable traffic. Started alongside the release and sized for that release's share alone, except on an adopted service's first release, where the instances already running are it. Not a control plane, and not what a rollback shifts traffic onto: a started control is sized for a share and would saturate. [The health monitor](how-the-factory-works/08-operations/01-the-health-monitor.md)
- **decision** — what a gate firing writes into the append-only log: an open event carrying the factor vector, and a second row naming it, either a close event carrying the verdict or an abandonment where no verdict will come. [Where a gate is, and what decides it](how-the-factory-works/03-gates/01-where-a-gate-is-and-what-decides-it.md)
- **detector** — anything of the factory's own that raises an intent: [the health monitor](how-the-factory-works/08-operations/01-the-health-monitor.md) still running after its window closed, a consumer breaking in its own error rate, a permanent constraint no intent names, an advisory naming a package a shipped build resolved. It raises one per condition, not per observation; later evidence attaches to the intent already raised. [Intake](how-the-factory-works/02-intent-into-items/01-intake/README.md)
- **Edit in place** — the gate action where a human edits the artifact under decision instead of rejecting the item to get a different one. Offered at Decomposition and at every artifact gate but Implementation. [Actions at each gate](how-the-factory-works/03-gates/03-actions-at-each-gate.md)
- **gate** — a decision point that writes an open event and the row that ends it into the chained log: eight at the stage boundaries an item passes, one over a role prompt or skill version, and one over a safeguard's withdrawal. Not a checkpoint a human staffs: the score auto-passes what it judges low risk, and a human is at one only where a threshold, a safeguard, or a resolved factor sent them. [Where a gate is, and what decides it](how-the-factory-works/03-gates/01-where-a-gate-is-and-what-decides-it.md)
- **the graph** — the one linked set of records the product is built around: intent, item, spec, build, gate decision, deploy, incident, and score, joined by links rather than matched across systems. [Tight integration](what-the-factory-does/01-tight-integration.md)
- **in force** — of a versioned record the factory writes, the version agents currently work from: the newest its gate approved and nobody has withdrawn. Not the legal sense, and never a field — it is read off the chain.
- **hold** — a stop that leaves the event queued with the change still good, offered at the deploy rows alone. Three kinds, and only a human's is written as a decision. [What a gate may change](how-the-factory-works/03-gates/04-what-a-gate-may-change.md)
- **mitigation** — narrowed to the two recorded operations the deployer performs on a human's instruction from Ops: shifting traffic off a target, and changing a deployed release's instance count. Not the field's whole class of impact-reducing action — nothing here makes a build or restores one outside a window. [Deferred, but not designed out](deferred.md#security-comes-last)
- **incident** — a record on a production environment, written by the health monitor against the release running there, never by a human. [Incidents](how-the-factory-works/08-operations/06-incidents.md)
- **intent** — what arrives and what everything links back to: one record from three sources — an owner's request, a group of end-user reports, or something the factory found. [Intake](how-the-factory-works/02-intent-into-items/01-intake/README.md)
- **item** — the unit the factory moves: one thing that can ship by itself, one timeline, one release. [One pipeline](how-the-factory-works/01-one-pipeline.md)
- **organic traffic** — the requests real users are already making against a production environment, as against load an encoding generates or one human exercising a screen. Not web analytics' unpaid search referrals. [The rollout strategy](how-the-factory-works/03-gates/02-the-rollout-strategy.md)
- **page** — the narrow channel, for a wait where the deployed software is worse until a human ends it. The only channel that widens and the only one that writes a record. [Pages](how-the-factory-works/08-operations/07-pages.md)
- **the pipeline** — the one path every item takes: the stages that author it and the gate at each boundary. There is no second path and no bypass. [One pipeline](how-the-factory-works/01-one-pipeline.md)
- **release** — the name a build has on master, from merge onward, and the record where the graph joins. [The release record](how-the-factory-works/06-releases/02-the-release-record.md)
- **rollback** — a deploy event that shifts traffic onto the instances of the release it returns to, still running, minting and retiring no number. [Rollback](how-the-factory-works/06-releases/06-rollback.md)
- **seam** — a boundary declared between two writers, or between the factory and something not designed yet, so that neither side reaches past it. [_Deferred, but not designed out_](deferred.md) names and numbers five of the second kind: the actor, the log, the credential resolver, the deploy target, and the principal on a call. [_Records and their writers_](records.md) is where the first kind is enumerated: it holds a row per record, and names the seam in the row of each record two writers reach. [Deferred, but not designed out](deferred.md)
- **search** — what recovers attribution when the batched deploy of a revert fails its window: builds made and deployed one at a time, each with a control and a window of its own, bisecting the batch by release number. Not a query or a lookup: it runs under a search budget, and it can end naming nobody. [Overlapping windows](how-the-factory-works/08-operations/03-overlapping-windows.md)
- **service** — one repository with one long-lived branch, and no repository holds two. Master, the release number, and the queue's ordering are all per service. [Decomposition](how-the-factory-works/02-intent-into-items/03-decomposition/README.md)
- **skipped** — the [analysis window](how-the-factory-works/08-operations/02-the-analysis-window.md)'s exit taken when a rollback undoes a release it was not aimed at, which happens because a rollback undoes every release above the one it returns to. The release is left neither failed nor running, with none of its own instances to return to. Not a check that did not run. [The analysis window](how-the-factory-works/08-operations/02-the-analysis-window.md)
- **store** — a contract whose consumer is the service's own past, which is why it promises forward as well as backward. The bare word takes this sense; the artifact store and the report store are components, named in full wherever they appear, and neither is this line. [The store is a contract too](how-the-factory-works/07-contracts/09-the-store-is-a-contract-too.md)

- **hazard severity** — graded by what the factory can do about the harm afterwards, `negligible`, `recoverable`, or `irreversible`, where safety engineering grades severity by the magnitude of the harm and keeps recoverability apart as controllability; the magnitude enters instead as the bound an owner authors on an `irreversible` area's hazardous operation.
