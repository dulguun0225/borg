## Still to write

**Spend is priced but never bounded.** Two rows of [_Gate policy_](how-humans-do-it/09-gate-policy.md#what-it-holds) state their cost in spend — the attempt bound "burns spend before anyone sees the item", the interview's round bound "spends round after round on one that never will" — and Factory carries cost per feature on its readout. Nothing caps spend. The attempt bound and the round bound are the only things standing between a circling item and unbounded cost, and neither is named as that. Whether a spend ceiling is a ninth parameter or the two bounds are deliberately the whole answer is unsettled, and it is an owner's call, so it belongs in [_Open_](open.md) phrased as the question — not resolved by asserting either answer in the body.

**A role and a scope are still two words.** [_The fleet_](how-humans-do-it/10-fleet.md) settles what stands behind an agent — the account, the credential, who paid, and what a spent or withdrawn one costs — and leaves the other half of [_One pipeline_](how-humans-do-it/01-one-pipeline.md)'s definition where it found it. Nothing enumerates the roles or says what a scope bounds. Both are load-bearing: a scope is what seam 4 of [_Deferred, but not designed out_](deferred.md) attaches policy to, and the roles are what [_Dispatch_](how-humans-do-it/02-intent-into-items.md#dispatch) selects between at a decision it deliberately gives no gate. Enumerating roles risks inventing a taxonomy the document does not need, so the work is to say what a role has to be for dispatch to choose one, and what a scope has to bound for the seam to hold it — not to list them.

**Adoption of an existing codebase is never designed.** The single mention is in [_What a diff cannot see_](how-humans-do-it/07-contracts.md#what-a-diff-cannot-see): an interface adopted from outside the factory arrives without declared meaning, and retrofitting the name is itself a breaking change. Everything else assumes a factory that has always been there — contracts declared with modes, a design system chosen, an authorship prior with history behind it, a production deploy record to be the current release, a control standing for the first comparison. A first run has none of them, and the score starting wide covers only the authorship half. Largest of the three, and it may be [_Deferred, but not designed out_](deferred.md) material rather than a section: the question to settle first is whether adoption is designed now or named as sequenced away, with what it would cost to retrofit stated either way.
